Design notes

  • No USB, CAN or DAC; 2 USART, 1 I2C, 1 SPI/I2S.
  • ADC external channels: 19 on LQFP48, 16 on LQFP32, 13 on TSSOP20.
  • Internal 48 MHz RC means no crystal is needed; supply is 2.0-3.6 V (5 V tolerant I/Os).
