Design notes

  • SAM-BA bootloader in ROM over UART0 or USB (USB boot needs a 12 or 16 MHz crystal); an erased part boots SAM-BA. Assert the ERASE pin to get back to it.
  • Order revision B parts (…B-AN): CAN-FD is ISO 11898-1:2015 compliant on rev B. KiCad only has symbols for the legacy rev A (ATSAME70x21A-AN, same pinout).
  • The 64-pin ATSAME70J has no USB, only one CAN, 5 ADC inputs and no SD/MMC; the N (100-pin) has 1 SPI, 1 I2SC and 10 ADC inputs.
  • uart = 3 USART + 5 UART; i2s = SSC + 2 I2SC. LCD = 8080-style module via the 16-bit SMC (no RGB controller).
  • USB High-Speed needs VDDIO >= 3.0 V. Ethernet MAC (MII/RMII) needs an external PHY.
