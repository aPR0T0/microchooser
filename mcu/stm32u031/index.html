Design notes

  • 4 USART + 2 LPUART, 3 I2C, 2 SPI (no I2S); no USB or segment LCD (see STM32U073/U083).
  • Standby with RTC is 0.16 uA at 1.8 V (about 0.24 uA at 3 V); shutdown 16 nA.
  • Touch channels: 12 on LQFP48, 7 on UFQFPN32, 3 on TSSOP20.
