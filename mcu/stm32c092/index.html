Design notes

  • One FDCAN controller (needs an external CAN transceiver); 30 KB RAM because 6 KB is used as FDCAN message RAM (the CAN-less C091 has 36 KB).
  • No USB (see STM32C071) and no DAC.
  • The ROM bootloader supports FDCAN except on the TSSOP-20 package.
