Design notes

  • CAN FD (up to 8 Mbit/s data phase) only on CH32L103C8T6 and CH32L103K8U6; other packages have classic CAN 2.0B.
  • K8U6/F8U6 have built-in 5.1k Rd resistors for Type-C sink. USB is one FS controller (host or device).
  • Small packages lose peripherals: K8U6/F8P6 have 1 SPI and 1 I2C.
  • Factory USB/UART (USART2) ISP bootloader via BOOT0. Debug is WCH 2-wire SDI via WCH-LinkE.
  • K8U6 was out of stock at LCSC when checked (Nov 2026).
