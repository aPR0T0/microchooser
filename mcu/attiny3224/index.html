Design notes

  • Unlike the 1-series it has no DAC, no TCD and no touch (PTC), but gets a second USART and a 12-bit differential ADC with 1-16x PGA.
  • 12 GPIO counts the UPDI/RESET pin.
  • Pin-compatible 20/24-pin siblings: ATtiny3226 / ATtiny3227.
  • Blank chips are flashed over the 1-wire UPDI pin; a USB-serial adapter plus a resistor (SerialUPDI) or any cheap UPDI programmer works.
