Design notes

  • Only 512 bytes of SRAM (ramKB rounds to 0).
  • No hardware UART; I2C and SPI share the single USI (Universal Serial Interface), which needs software assistance - use one at a time.
  • 6 I/O only if RESET is disabled as GPIO (then high-voltage programming is needed to reflash); 5 I/O normally.
  • Blank chips need an ISP programmer; the -20 grade runs 2.7-5.5 V (ATtiny85V: 1.8 V at up to 10 MHz).
  • The SOIC is the 208-mil (5.3 mm) wide-body version, not standard 150-mil SOIC-8.
  • For new designs the ATtiny412 / ATtiny202 (tinyAVR 1/0-series) are cheaper and add a real USART.
