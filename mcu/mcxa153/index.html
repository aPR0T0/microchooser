Design notes

  • This Cortex-M33 has NO FPU, DSP extension, TrustZone or MPU.
  • USB is full-speed DEVICE only (crystal-less), no host.
  • ROM ISP over LPUART and USB (SPI/I2C ISP not available on A14x/A15x) - no programmer needed.
  • No RTC peripheral (wake timer / OS event timer only).
  • Also has 1x I3C (can talk to legacy I2C devices), eQDC quadrature decoder and FlexPWM with 6 outputs; ADC inputs depend on package (fewer on QFN-32).
  • MCXA152 = 64 KB flash / 16 KB RAM; MCXA14x = 48 MHz versions. FRDM-MCXA153 is the low-cost dev board.
