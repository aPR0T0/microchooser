Design notes

  • 4 USART + 4 UART; 3 classic bxCAN (not CAN-FD); 6 SPI on 144/176-pin (fewer on LQFP100).
  • 512 KB RAM = 368 KB SRAM1 + 16 KB SRAM2 + 128 KB DTCM, plus 16 KB ITCM; FMC supports SDRAM (Bank1 only on LQFP100).
  • USB OTG_HS needs an external ULPI PHY for 480 Mbit/s (internal FS PHY).
  • Ethernet needs an external PHY (MII/RMII).
  • No crypto accelerator (the STM32F777 adds AES/HASH).
