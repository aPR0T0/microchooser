Design notes

  • Cortex-M4 without FPU (DSP instructions only).
  • USB OTG FS with on-chip PHY and a crystal-less mode; ROM bootloader supports USB DFU and USART1/USART2.
  • 48/32-pin parts have 3 USARTs and 10 ADC channels; 5 UARTs and 16 ADC channels need LQFP-64. A 7x7 mm LQFP-64 (-7 suffix) also exists.
  • Not register-compatible with STM32; use Artery's firmware library. Standard Arm SWD/JTAG probes work.
