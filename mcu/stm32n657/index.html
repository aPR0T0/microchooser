Design notes

  • No internal flash: code boots from external XSPI NOR flash (secure boot ROM loads it into SRAM or runs XIP).
  • Only BGA packages (smallest pitch 0.5 mm on VFBGA142/223); VFBGA264 at 0.8 mm is the easiest to route.
  • Smaller packages lose peripherals: VFBGA142 (Z0) has no SDMMC, 2 USART + 3 UART and no 16-bit XSPI.
  • Two USB 2.0 OTG HS controllers with on-chip HS PHYs (one with UCPD); 5 USART + 5 UART + 1 LPUART, 2x I3C.
  • N657 includes crypto (AES/SAES/PKA/MCE); the N647 is the same chip without the NPU.
