Design notes

  • Only 4 KB SRAM.
  • ROM bootstrap loader (BSL) over UART or I2C, invoked with the BSL pin at reset, so no programmer is strictly needed.
  • No RTC peripheral, no DAC output and no crystal oscillator (internal ±1.2% SYSOSC and 32 kHz LFOSC only). STANDBY figure is with a 32 kHz timer running.
  • Analog extras: 2 zero-drift chopper op-amps with PGA, 1 general-purpose amp, 1 fast comparator with 8-bit reference DAC.
  • No upstream Zephyr board and no mature Rust HAL yet.
