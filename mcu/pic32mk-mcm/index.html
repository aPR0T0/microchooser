Design notes

  • 4 CAN FD controllers (no classic-only CAN); the related PIC32MK MCF parts have 4 classic CAN 2.0B instead. External transceivers required.
  • 2 full-speed USB OTG controllers, 4 op-amps, 5 comparators, 3 DACs, 6 QEI, 12 motor-control PWM generators (24 outputs).
  • Seven 12-bit SAR ADC cores; 42 analog inputs on 100-pin (26 on 64-pin).
  • Specs taken from Microchip's official PIC32MK-MC device pack (datasheet DS60001519); FPU listed there as single precision.
  • No ROM bootloader: needs an ICSP/JTAG programmer (PICkit 4/5, ICD). Not supported by Arduino/PlatformIO.
