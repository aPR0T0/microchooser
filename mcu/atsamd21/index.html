Design notes

  • No ROM bootloader: blank chips must be flashed once over SWD (e.g. to install an Arduino/UF2 bootloader); afterwards USB uploads work.
  • uart/i2c/spi counts are SERCOMs: each SERCOM is either a USART, I2C or SPI, so the totals are shared (e.g. 6 SERCOMs = 3 UART + 2 I2C + 1 SPI). SERCOMs: 4 on E (32-pin), 6 on G/J.
  • ADC inputs 10/14/20 and PTC self-cap touch channels 6/10/16 (up to 256 mutual-cap) for E/G/J packages.
  • USB full-speed device + embedded host; can run crystal-less from the internal 48 MHz DFLL locked to USB SOF.
  • No FPU and only 32 KB RAM: fine for CircuitPython/Arduino, tight for big buffers.
