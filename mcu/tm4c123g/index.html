Design notes

  • USB 2.0 full-speed OTG with internal PHY; two CAN 2.0 A/B controllers (no CAN-FD, external transceivers needed).
  • 3.3 V only (3.15-3.63 V supply). 2 KB EEPROM; two 1 Msps ADCs sharing 12 inputs; 2 QEI.
  • PWM count is the two dedicated PWM modules (16 outputs); the 12 general-purpose timers can add more.
  • ROM boot loader (UART0, SSI0, I2C0, USB DFU) runs automatically on blank flash; afterwards only if a GPIO is set up in BOOTCFG.
  • Sleep figure is Hibernate with RTC (SRAM not retained; 16 battery-backed words). Deep-sleep with RAM retention is about 1 mA.
  • Pricey for an M4F today (about $14+). Energia is discontinued; PlatformIO titiva supports the EK-TM4C123GXL. Zephyr's ek_tm4c123gxl support is minimal and Rust (tm4c123x-hal) is community-maintained.
