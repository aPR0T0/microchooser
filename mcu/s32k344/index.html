Design notes

  • All 6 FlexCAN controllers support CAN-FD.
  • Cortex-M7 in lockstep (ASIL D) with single-precision FPU; 512 KB SRAM incl. 192 KB TCM, plus 128 KB data flash. HSE_B security engine (AES, secure boot).
  • Feature letter: 'E' parts (S32K344EH...) have a 10/100 Ethernet MAC + 2 SAI; 'N' parts have neither.
  • Single 2.97-5.5 V supply (3.3 V or 5 V systems). No USB.
  • Packages: 172-pin HDQFP (16x16 mm, two staggered rows of 0.65 mm-pitch leads) or 257 MAPBGA (14x14 mm, 0.8 mm); the data sheet also lists a 100-pin LQFP.
  • No serial ROM bootloader: program over SWD/JTAG; handle the HSE firmware/lifecycle carefully.
  • ADC (3x 12-bit SAR, up to 24 inputs each), GPIO and eMIOS PWM counts are approximate/conservative - check the IO muxing sheet for the exact package.
  • 16 LPUARTs all LIN-capable; FlexIO (32 ch) can emulate extra UART/SPI/I2C/I2S/SENT.
