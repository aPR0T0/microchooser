Design notes

  • No USB, no CAN. The x8 (64 KB) parts get a 2nd I2C, 2nd SPI and an extra timer; x4 parts have 1 USART.
  • Cortex-M23 (Armv8-M Baseline) rather than M0+, so check toolchain/RTOS support; also offered in a tiny 3x3 mm LGA-20.
  • ROM bootloader on USART0 (PA9/PA10) or USART1 (PA14/PA15) via BOOT0; standard Arm SWD probes work.
