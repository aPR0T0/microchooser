Design notes

  • Huge family (16 KB-512 KB); figures here cover the listed parts. 256/512 KB M031xG/xI parts run at 72 MHz and add RTC, QSPI and more UARTs. M032 adds crystal-less USB FS device.
  • Nuvoton calls the 4x4 mm QFN 'QFN33' (exposed pad = pin 33, VSS). The x8+ parts add a USCI (configurable UART/SPI/I2C) and 2 comparators; no RTC on these densities.
  • ISP needs a loader in LDROM (Nuvoton supplies UART/USB ISP code); plan on a Nu-Link (SWD) for the first program since blank-chip ROM ISP is not documented.
  • TSSOP-20 part had very low LCSC stock when checked.
