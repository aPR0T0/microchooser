Design notes

  • SiP with integrated LTE-M/NB-IoT modem (3GPP Rel. 14, bands 1-5, 8, 12, 13, 17-20, 25, 26, 28, 65, 66, 85), GNSS receiver, RF front end and PMIC. Needs a SIM (nano-SIM/eSIM/iSIM), an LTE antenna and a GNSS antenna.
  • Power Class 3 (23 dBm) and Class 5 (20 dBm). DECT NR+ needs separate modem firmware; NTN (satellite) bands are being added.
  • No Bluetooth/Thread radio. Pair it with an nRF52/nRF54L if you need BLE.
  • 4 serial boxes, each UARTE, SPIM or TWIM, so max is 4 of any one type (counts are shared). VBAT 3.0-5.5 V; I/O voltage comes from VDD_GPIO (1.8-3.3 V). TX current peaks need a stiff supply.
  • No ROM bootloader; flash over SWD. The modem firmware is a separate Nordic binary, and the app uses the nRF Modem library (nRF Connect SDK).
  • PSM floor current is not listed here because the official figure couldn't be confirmed. LCSC stock is often zero; DigiKey/Mouser or the Nordic Thingy:91 X / nRF9151 DK are alternatives for prototyping.
