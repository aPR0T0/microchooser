Design notes

  • Peripheral counts scale with pin count: USART 3/3/5/6, I2C 1/2/2/2, ADC inputs 10/14/18/22, PTC self-cap channels 18/22/32/46 for 28/32/48/64 pins.
  • GPIO counts include the input-only PF6/RESET pin.
  • Internal 24 MHz oscillator with auto-tune from a 32 kHz crystal; no crystal needed.
  • Blank chips are flashed over the 1-wire UPDI pin; a USB-serial adapter plus a resistor (SerialUPDI) or any cheap UPDI programmer works.
  • Arduino support via the community DxCore.
