Design notes

  • 192 KB RAM includes 64 KB CCM RAM (CPU-only, not DMA accessible).
  • Ethernet needs an external PHY (e.g. LAN8720 over RMII).
  • USB OTG_HS runs at full speed with its internal PHY; 480 Mbit/s needs an external ULPI PHY.
  • 4 USART + 2 UART; classic bxCAN (not CAN-FD); FSMC usable as an 8080 parallel LCD interface.
  • No crypto accelerator (only RNG); the STM32F417 adds AES/HASH.
