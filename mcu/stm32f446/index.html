Design notes

  • I2C count = 3 standard I2C + 1 FMPI2C (Fast-mode Plus).
  • USB OTG_HS runs at full speed with its internal PHY; 480 Mbit/s needs an external ULPI PHY.
  • 4 USART + 2 UART; classic bxCAN (not CAN-FD); no Ethernet.
  • LQFP64 loses SPI4 and FMC and has a limited Quad-SPI; FMC on LQFP100 is Bank1 only (8080 LCD/SRAM, no SDRAM); full FMC on 144-pin.
  • No RNG and no crypto.
