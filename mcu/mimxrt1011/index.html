Design notes

  • Flashless: needs an external QSPI NOR flash on FlexSPI (XIP). 128 KB FlexRAM is shared between ITCM/DTCM/OCRAM.
  • ROM serial downloader over USB (HID) and LPUART1 - flash with NXP MCUXpresso Secure Provisioning / blhost / MCUBootUtility, no programmer needed.
  • USB 2.0 OTG with integrated high-speed PHY; 24 MHz crystal required.
  • Single 3.3 V supply into the on-chip DCDC (needs external inductor); I/O banks can be 1.8 V or 3.3 V.
  • No CAN, Ethernet or SD host. 2x SAI + SPDIF + MQS for audio; DCP (AES-128/SHA-256) and HAB secure boot.
  • MIMXRT1011DAE5A is consumer grade (0-95 C Tj, 500 MHz); MIMXRT1011CAE4A is industrial (400 MHz).
  • External-flash limit is the approximate FlexSPI memory-mapped window.
