Design notes

  • BLE 5.0 without 2M PHY, Coded PHY or extended advertising.
  • Small ecosystem: no official Arduino or MicroPython; use the Bouffalo SDK or Zephyr (bl60x, Ai-WB2-12F kit).
  • SDIO is slave-only (no SD-card host); no I2S and no USB - flash over UART with the Bouffalo flasher (ROM UART/SDIO boot).
  • BL602C-00 has no internal flash and needs an external QSPI flash; the -20 parts have 2 MB in package.
  • Supply range 2.1-3.63 V per datasheet (VDDIO can run at 1.8 V); needs a 24/26/32/38.4/40 MHz crystal.
  • Ai-WB2-12F uses the ESP-12F outline (16x24 mm, 22 pads, 2 mm pitch) but its pinout differs.
