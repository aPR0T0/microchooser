Design notes

  • SERCOMs (4 on 48-pin, 2 on 32-pin) are each USART/SPI, I2C on 3 of the 4 - totals are shared. No USB, no CAN, no touch controller.
  • 29 GPIO on the 48-pin part only when the 32 kHz crystal is unused (27 otherwise); 16 on the 32-pin part.
  • TX power: up to +12 dBm (BZ25048) / ~+5 dBm (BZ24032). Needs RF matching/antenna design - the pre-certified WBZ451 (PCB antenna) / WBZ451H (+20 dBm) modules avoid this.
  • Deep sleep 1.7 uA with 8 KB backup RAM retained (1.46 uA without). Crypto: AES (incl. GCM), SHA, ECC/RSA (PUKCC), TRNG.
  • Matter is not listed in the data sheet; Thread is supported via Microchip's OpenThread stack.
  • No documented ROM bootloader: program over SWD.
