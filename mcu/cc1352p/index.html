Design notes

  • Integrated +20 dBm PA (63 mA at 915 MHz, 85 mA at 2.4 GHz); the RF matching network decides which band gets the high-power path (see LAUNCHXL-CC1352P1 vs -P-2).
  • Sub-GHz stacks: TI 15.4-Stack, Wi-SUN, Amazon Sidewalk, mioty, Wireless M-Bus, KNX RF; -121 dBm long-range mode. Not LoRa-compatible.
  • Not a Matter part (TI's Matter-capable dual-band chip is the CC1354P10).
  • 26 GPIO (fewer than the CC1352R because PA pins are used).
  • Third-party modules: Ebyte E79-900DM2005S (868/915 MHz) and E79-400DM2005S (433 MHz), 32x20 mm.
  • ROM serial bootloader over UART or SPI; debug is 2-pin cJTAG by default.
