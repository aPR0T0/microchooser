Design notes

  • Marked NRND by Espressif; the ESP32-C2/C3 are the suggested replacements.
  • I2C and PWM are software-implemented (any GPIO via Arduino Wire/analogWrite) - counted as 0 hardware channels here.
  • UART1 is TX-only (typically used for logs). ADC is a single 10-bit input (0-1 V on the bare chip).
  • RAM: 80 KB data RAM + 32 KB IRAM; only ~50 KB heap is left with Wi-Fi connected.
  • Strapping pins GPIO0, GPIO2 and GPIO15; deep-sleep wake needs GPIO16 tied to RST. Needs a USB-UART bridge to flash.
  • Bare chip needs external SPI flash, a 26/40 MHz crystal and RF matching; ESP-12F is the usual module (Ai-Thinker).
  • I2C and PWM are implemented in software (Arduino Wire / analogWrite work fine); counted here as 1 I2C bus and 4 PWM outputs.
