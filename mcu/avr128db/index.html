AVR128DB

Microchip, AVR DB. 5 V AVR with multi-voltage I/O (talk to 1.8/3.3 V parts on one port), built-in op-amps and a crystal oscillator - good for mixed-signal and level-shifting jobs.

Design notes

  • MVIO: PORTC runs from a separate VDDIO2 rail (1.8-5.5 V) for built-in level shifting.
  • 2 op-amps (28/32-pin) or 3 op-amps (48/64-pin); no PTC touch controller (unlike AVR DA).
  • Peripheral counts scale with pin count: USART 3/3/5/6, I2C 1/2/2/2, ADC inputs 9/13/18/22 for 28/32/48/64 pins.
  • GPIO counts include the input-only PF6/RESET pin.
  • Blank chips are flashed over the 1-wire UPDI pin; a USB-serial adapter plus a resistor (SerialUPDI) or any cheap UPDI programmer works.
  • Arduino support via the community DxCore.
