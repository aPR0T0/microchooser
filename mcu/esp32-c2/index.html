Design notes

  • Only sold with 2 or 4 MB flash in the package; there is no external flash or PSRAM interface.
  • Wi-Fi is 20 MHz-only 802.11n (max 72 Mbps). Needs a 26 or 40 MHz crystal.
  • No native USB: flash over UART0 with a USB-UART bridge; strapping pins GPIO8 and GPIO9.
  • Arduino-ESP32 supports the C2 only as an ESP-IDF component / custom lib build, not from the board manager.
  • The 'X' parts (chip revision v2.0) replace the original ESP8684H2/H4 and ESP8684-MINI-1-H4.
