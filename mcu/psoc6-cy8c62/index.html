PSoC 62 (CY8C62xx)

Infineon, PSoC 6. Dual-core 150 MHz Cortex-M4F + Cortex-M0+ with up to 2 MB flash / 1 MB SRAM, CapSense touch, USB FS, SD host, QSPI XIP and crypto; a low-power, touch-capable MCU for IoT. It has no radio.

Design notes

  • No radio. PSoC 63 is the BLE line, and Infineon's Wi-Fi/BT kits pair a PSoC 62 with an external CYW43xxx combo chip.
  • Serial ports come from SCBs that are each UART, SPI or I2C at runtime, so the uart/i2c/spi counts are per-type maxima, not additive. CY8C62x8/xA has 13 SCBs: 8 UART/SPI/I2C, 4 UART/I2C only, and 1 Deep Sleep SPI/I2C. CY8C62x7 has 9 SCBs (8 + 1 Deep Sleep) and CY8C62x5 has 7 (6 + 1 Deep Sleep).
  • Peripherals differ by sub-line. CAN FD (1 block) exists only on CY8C62x5 (-S3D6x/-S3D7x parts). SD host: 2 on CY8C62x8/xA, 1 on 62x5, none on 62x7. I2S: 2 on 62x8/xA, 1 on 62x7, none on 62x5. The 12-bit DAC, 2 opamps and 12 UDBs are only on CY8C62x7.
  • USB is full-speed device/host only, with no high-speed PHY.
  • UDBs (62x7 only) and Smart I/O give some programmable logic, but they are not comparable to RP2040 PIO.
  • There is no ROM UART/USB bootloader, so blank chips must be flashed over SWD (KitProg3, MiniProg4 or J-Link). DFU/MCUboot can be added in firmware.
  • The Infineon Arduino core (arduino-core-psoc6) only supports the CY8CKIT-062S2-AI board (CY8C624ABZI-S2D44). Infineon's MicroPython port for PSoC 6 lives in Infineon's fork, not upstream MicroPython.
  • activeUaPerMhz is the CM4 at 1.1 V core (LP mode, needed for 150 MHz). The 0.9 V ULP mode (up to 50 MHz) gives 22-27 uA/MHz.
  • CapSense works on any GPIO except the dedicated CMOD capacitor pin, so touchChannels is the GPIO count minus one.
  • SMIF supports single, dual and quad SPI (octal and dual-quad on 62x7/62x8/62xA) with XIP and on-the-fly AES. externalFlashMaxMB assumes the 128 MB XIP window.
