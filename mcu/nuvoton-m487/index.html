Design notes

  • USB 2.0 HS OTG with on-chip PHY on all parts; the extra USB FS OTG controller is only on LQFP-128/144 (M487SIDAE has HS only).
  • Ethernet is a 10/100 MAC with RMII (external PHY required). Crypto: AES, DES/3DES, SHA/HMAC, ECC, RNG.
  • SPIM maps external quad-SPI flash with a 32 KB cache; the maximum size is not stated in the datasheet (16 MB assumed). EBI provides an 8/16-bit LCD parallel bus.
  • Datasheet mentions a 32 KB ROM boot loader with native ISP, but interfaces are not documented there; keep a Nu-Link/SWD probe handy. LCSC stock is low and prices are high (~$6.5+).
  • Nuvoton maintains a MicroPython fork (OpenNuvoton) for NuMaker-IoT-M487; not upstream.
