Design notes

  • Very small memory: 16 KB flash and only 1 KB SRAM.
  • No ROM bootloader (BSL) on the C-series: blank chips must be flashed over SWD (XDS110, J-Link or CMSIS-DAP with OpenOCD/pyOCD).
  • Internal oscillators only (24 MHz SYSOSC, 32 kHz LFOSC); no crystal, RTC, comparator or DAC.
  • 8-pin packages (SOT-23-8, WSON-8, WCSP) expose only 6 GPIO; the 20-pin VSSOP gives 18. The 20-pin TSSOP is sold as MSPS003F4, not MSPM0C1104.
  • PWM count (14) is TI's figure and includes the advanced timer's complementary outputs.
  • No mature Rust HAL yet (embassy MSPM0 support is still in development).
