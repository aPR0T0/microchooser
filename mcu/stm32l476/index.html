Design notes

  • 128 KB SRAM = 96 KB SRAM1 + 32 KB SRAM2 (SRAM2 can be retained in Standby).
  • 3 USART + 2 UART + 1 LPUART; audio via 2x SAI (no I2S peripheral).
  • Segment LCD driver up to 8x40 (8x28 on LQFP64) with internal step-up converter; touch channels 12 / 21 / 24 on 64 / 100 / 144-pin.
  • No HSI48: USB OTG FS needs an HSE crystal or LSE-trimmed MSI; VDDUSB is a separate 3.0-3.6 V supply.
  • FMC (static memories / 8080 LCD) only on 100-pin and larger packages.
