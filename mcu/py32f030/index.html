Design notes

  • K1x/K2x and F1x/F2x are two different pinouts of the same die (K2 = 30 GPIO, K1 = 28 GPIO); check the pinout table before copying an STM32F030 layout.
  • Extras: 4-digit 7-segment LED scan driver, 4 high-sink (up to 80 mA) GPIOs, LSE crystal + RTC, 3-channel DMA, 2 comparators. No USB.
  • Flash over UART with the ROM bootloader (BOOT0 high at reset, any USB-serial adapter) or over standard Arm SWD; OpenOCD/pyOCD need the Puya device pack.
  • Datasheets and reference manuals are mostly Chinese; community GCC template: github.com/IOsetting/py32f0-template.
