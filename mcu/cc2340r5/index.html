Design notes

  • No USB and no I2S; only 1 UART, 1 I2C and 1 SPI.
  • CC2340R52 has 36 KB SRAM, CC2340R53 has 64 KB (same 512 KB flash). Standby figure is for the R52 with RTC and full RAM retention.
  • Qualified against Bluetooth Core 5.4 (older listings say 5.3). Zigbee via the F3 SDK, Thread via TI's OpenThread SDK; no Matter support.
  • Max TX power +8 dBm; needs a 48 MHz crystal. QFN-24 version exposes only 12 GPIO.
  • ROM serial bootloader over UART or SPI.
  • Third-party module: RF-star RF-BM-2340A2 (CC2340R52 in QFN-24, LCSC C20624105, about $4).
