Design notes

  • USB is full-speed device only. Factory ISP runs over USB-HID (hold P3.2 low at power-up) or UART with the STC-ISP tool; no programmer needed. Newer (B/C/D) silicon also supports USB debugging.
  • RAM is 8 KB xdata + 256 B idata (+1.25 KB USB buffer). 8-channel advanced PWM (PWMA/PWMB, with dead time); no hardware DAC.
  • LCM interface drives 8080/6800 TFT panels (not on 'A' silicon). Has RTC, DMA, 16-bit MDU and a comparator.
  • Internal oscillator up to 45 MHz, no crystal needed even for USB. Documentation is primarily Chinese; English datasheet available.
