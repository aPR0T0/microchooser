Design notes

  • Blank chips have no bootloader: burn the Arduino (Optiboot) bootloader once with an ISP programmer (or another Arduino as ISP), then upload over a USB-serial adapter.
  • 20 MHz needs 4.5-5.5 V; at 3.3 V stay at or below ~13 MHz (8 MHz internal RC is common).
  • ADC6/ADC7 (analog-only) exist only on the TQFP/QFN packages; DIP-28 has 6 ADC inputs.
  • The USART can also act as a second SPI master (MSPIM).
  • RTC = Timer2 asynchronous 'real-time counter' with a 32.768 kHz crystal on the XTAL pins (then the core must run from the internal RC).
  • The ATmega328PB (TQFP/QFN only) adds a 2nd USART, SPI and I2C, but is not a drop-in replacement (former VCC/GND pins become GPIO).
