ATtiny412

Microchip, tinyAVR 1-series. Tiny, cheap 8-pin SOIC AVR with a real USART, I2C, SPI, DAC and event system; runs at 5 V and programs over a single UPDI wire.

Design notes

  • Only 256 bytes of SRAM and 4 KB flash (ramKB rounds to 0).
  • 6 GPIO counts the UPDI/RESET pin; using it as GPIO makes reprogramming need a 12 V UPDI high-voltage programmer, so plan on 5 usable I/O.
  • DAC is 8-bit, one output. USART, I2C and SPI pins overlap heavily on 8 pins - check the mux table.
  • Blank chips are flashed over the 1-wire UPDI pin; a USB-serial adapter plus a resistor (SerialUPDI) or any cheap UPDI programmer works.
  • Arduino support via the community megaTinyCore.
