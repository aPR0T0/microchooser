Design notes

  • No USB, no CAN, no DAC; 2 USART, 1 I2C, 1 SPI/I2S.
  • SO-8 version has only 6 usable I/Os (pins are multi-bonded).
  • Runs from 2.0-3.6 V only (not 5 V) despite targeting 8-bit replacement; I/Os are 5 V tolerant.
  • Internal 48 MHz RC (HSI48) means no crystal is needed.
  • Low-power is modest: Standby about 7.5 uA (RTC off in Standby/Shutdown), Shutdown 19 nA; ROM bootloader is USART/I2C.
