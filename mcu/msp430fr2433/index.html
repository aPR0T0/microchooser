Design notes

  • 15.5 KB FRAM total (15 KB program + 512 B information memory); listed as 15 KB here.
  • Serial: eUSCI_A0/A1 each do UART or SPI, eUSCI_B0 does I2C or SPI. So max 2 UART, 1 I2C or 3 SPI, but they share the same 3 modules.
  • Only 4 PWM outputs reach pins (TA0.1/2, TA1.1/2); Timer_A2/A3 instances have no external outputs.
  • RTC is a 16-bit counter-only RTC (no calendar). Sleep figure is LPM3 with RTC and RAM retained; LPM3.5 drops to 0.73 µA (no RAM retention).
  • ROM BSL over UART or I2C needs an entry sequence on RST/TEST (blank devices enter it automatically).
  • Only VQFN-24 and DSBGA packages. Energia (Arduino-like) is discontinued; the PlatformIO timsp430 platform still supports the MSP-EXP430FR2433 LaunchPad.
