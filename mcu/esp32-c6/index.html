Design notes

  • Wi-Fi, BLE and 802.15.4 share one 2.4 GHz radio (time-division coexistence).
  • USB is the fixed-function Serial/JTAG controller (no HID/MSC). SDIO is slave-only (no SD-card host).
  • Counts include the LP UART and LP I2C. Two classic CAN 2.0 (TWAI) controllers.
  • QFN40 ESP32-C6 needs external flash; ESP32-C6FH4/FH8 (QFN32) have in-package flash but only 22 GPIO. 40 MHz crystal required.
  • Strapping pins GPIO4 (MTMS), GPIO5 (MTDI), GPIO8, GPIO9 and GPIO15; GPIO12/13 are USB D-/D+.
