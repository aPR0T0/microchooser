Design notes

  • "1 MB" RAM = 192 KB TCM (64 KB ITCM + 128 KB DTCM) + 864 KB SRAM split across domains + 4 KB backup; not one contiguous block.
  • USB OTG_HS (on-chip FS PHY, external ULPI PHY for 480 Mbit/s) + USB OTG_FS.
  • 4 USART + 4 UART + 1 LPUART; three 16-bit ADCs; JPEG codec and Chrom-ART on chip.
  • FMC supports SDRAM/SRAM/NOR/NAND for large external frame buffers.
  • Early silicon (rev Y) was limited to 400 MHz; rev V parts run at 480 MHz.
