Design notes

  • Only 512 bytes of SRAM (stored as ramKB 0 because the schema uses whole KB).
  • One USCI_A (UART/LIN/IrDA or SPI) and one USCI_B (I2C or SPI): 1 UART + 1 I2C, or up to 2 SPI if you give those up.
  • No RTC peripheral (use Timer_A with a 32 kHz crystal). PWM: 2x Timer_A3 give 4 usable PWM outputs.
  • Capacitive touch on any GPIO via the pin-oscillator (no dedicated touch hardware).
  • ROM BSL on P1.1 (TX) / P1.5 (RX) needs a special RST/TEST entry sequence; most people flash via a LaunchPad's eZ-FET over Spy-Bi-Wire.
  • Energia (Arduino-like) is discontinued; the PlatformIO timsp430 platform still supports the G2553 LaunchPad.
