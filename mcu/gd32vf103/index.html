GigaDevice, GD32VF103. Early mainstream RISC-V MCU (Longan Nano): 108 MHz, 128 KB flash, USB FS OTG with ROM DFU bootloader, 2x CAN and 5 UARTs in an STM32F103-like layout.

Design notes

  • Out of stock at LCSC/JLCPCB when checked (Nov 2026) and pricey for what it is; CH32V203/CH32V307 are cheaper RISC-V alternatives with similar peripherals.
  • ROM bootloader supports USB DFU (no programmer needed) plus USART0/USART1. Debug is RISC-V JTAG (e.g. Sipeed RV-Debugger, J-Link, FT2232).
  • Small packages lose peripherals: CB has 3 UARTs; TB (QFN-36) has 2 UARTs, 1 I2C, 1 SPI. EXMC only on LQFP-100.
  • Rust: gd32vf103xx-hal / longan-nano crates. PlatformIO 'gd32v' platform (Sipeed) also offers a basic Arduino framework.
