Design notes

  • No internal flash, no ADC, no USB and no radio: boards add SPI flash, a USB-UART bridge (flashed with kflash over the ROM UART ISP) and often an ESP32/ESP8285 for WiFi.
  • 8 MB on-chip SRAM = 6 MB general + 2 MB reserved for the KPU when AI is used. 400 MHz nominal (commonly overclocked to 600 MHz+).
  • Needs 0.9 V core plus 1.8 V/3.3 V I/O rails; I/O banks are 1.8 or 3.3 V. Fine-pitch 0.65 mm BGA-144, so most people use a module (Sipeed M1/M1W).
  • 48 pins are freely mappable via the FPIOA. SPI: 2 masters (quad/octal capable) + 1 slave-only SPI; SPI3 is dedicated to boot flash. Extras: FFT accelerator, AES/SHA256, 3x I2S with mic-array APU.
  • MicroPython means Sipeed's MaixPy port; Arduino means Maixduino. Successor is the Kendryte K230.
