Design notes

  • No Bluetooth at all, and no FPU (floating point is software-emulated).
  • Native USB 1.1 OTG (TinyUSB device/host); the ROM can flash over USB (DFU/CDC) as well as UART.
  • ESP32-S2FN4R2 has a larger exposed pad (4.6 mm) than the other QFN56 variants (4.0 mm).
  • Strapping pins GPIO0, GPIO45 and GPIO46; bare chips need a 40 MHz crystal and RF matching.
  • Datasheet quotes up to 1 GB external memory address space, but ESP-IDF supports at most 16 MB flash; up to 10.5 MB PSRAM can be mapped.
  • ESP32-S2-MINI-2 uses the same 65-pad land pattern as the MINI-1 (KiCad ESP32-S2-MINI-1 footprint).
