Design notes

  • Not a Matter part: TI's Matter support targets the larger-flash CC2652R7 / CC2674 devices.
  • Many popular Zigbee coordinator modules (Sonoff ZBDongle-P, Ebyte E72-2G4M20S1E) use the CC2652P (+20 dBm PA), not the CC2652R.
  • CC2652RSIP is TI's certified 7x7 mm system-in-package (crystals, balun and DC/DC parts inside) with 32 GPIO on a 73-pad LGA.
  • Autonomous 16-bit sensor controller (4 KB SRAM) for low-power sensing and capacitive touch; +5 dBm max TX.
  • ROM serial bootloader over UART or SPI; debug is 2-pin cJTAG by default (XDS110).
  • LCSC rarely stocks it (listed at broker prices); buy from DigiKey/Mouser.
