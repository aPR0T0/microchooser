Design notes

  • USB is device-only (full/low speed); CC pins support basic Type-C attach detection but no USB PD protocol.
  • 16 KB flash (14 KB usable when the factory bootloader is kept), 256 B iRAM + 1 KB xRAM. No hardware I2C (bit-bang it). ADC is 8-bit.
  • No debugger: flash over USB/UART with WCHISPTool, wchisp or ch55xduino. Bootloader is entered by pulling UDP (or P1.5 on newer bootloaders) at power-up.
  • 5 V mode: 3.7-5.5 V on VCC with V33 decoupled; 3.3 V mode: tie V33 to VCC (2.8-3.6 V). 24 MHz needs the 5 V supply.
  • Pin counts include RST, XI/XO and the USB pins. WCH now recommends CH543/CH548 for new designs and discourages CH552E.
