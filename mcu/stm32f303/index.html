Design notes

  • RAM figures include CCM RAM: xB 32+8 KB, xC 40+8 KB, xE 64+16 KB.
  • UART4/5 only on 64-pin and larger; 48-pin parts have 3 USART and 2 I2C; the third I2C is xD/xE only.
  • USB is FS device only (no crystal-less mode, needs HSE); bxCAN is classic, not FD.
  • The small F303x6/x8 (e.g. K8T6) is a different subfamily: no USB, 2 ADCs, 3 DAC channels.
