Design notes

  • 48-pin (UFQFPN48) parts have no SDIO and only 10 ADC inputs; SPI4 exists only on 100-pin packages.
  • F401xB/xC parts (e.g. CCU6) have only 64 KB RAM.
  • USB OTG FS needs an accurate clock: use an HSE crystal for reliable USB.
  • No CAN, no DAC; 3 USART, 3 I2C.
