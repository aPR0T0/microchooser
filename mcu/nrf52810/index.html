Design notes

  • No FPU and only 24 KB RAM: fine for a simple BLE peripheral, tight once the SoftDevice Controller and a real application are both in.
  • No USB, no NFC, no I2S. The QFN32 package only brings out 16 GPIOs.
  • Nordic has no ROM bootloader. Flash a blank chip over SWD (J-Link, CMSIS-DAP or a Pi Pico debugprobe).
  • Needs a 32 MHz crystal; the 32.768 kHz crystal is optional. Nordic's DC/DC needs an external inductor.
  • Bluetooth only (plus ANT and 2.4 GHz proprietary). The nRF52811 is the near pin-compatible step up with Thread/Zigbee and direction finding.
  • Ebyte E73-2G4M04S1A is a cheap pre-certified module, but it is often out of stock at LCSC.
