Design notes

  • The maximum CPU clock depends on the package: 480 MHz only on the 224-pin BGA (Tj up to 105 C). It is 400 MHz on the 176/144-pin LQFP and 169-pin BGA, and 360 MHz on the 100-pin LQFP (240 MHz above Tj 105 C).
  • Two CANFD channels (classic CAN and CAN-FD) on every package. External transceivers are required.
  • USBHS (480 Mbit/s, on-chip HS PHY, host/device) is only on the 224/169-pin BGA and 176-pin LQFP. The 144-pin and 100-pin LQFP have USBFS only. USB needs VCC of at least 3.0 V.
  • Two SDHI/MMC hosts and one 10/100 Ethernet MAC (MII/RMII, external PHY). OSPI (xSPI/HyperBus, 2 x 256 MB windows) is on all packages. The SDRAM controller (128 MB window) is only on the 224/176/169-pin packages.
  • 6 SCI channels (UART, simple SPI or simple I2C, shared) plus 2 dedicated IIC, 1 I3C (counted as i2c) and 2 SPI. SSIE (I2S): 2, or 1 on 100-pin.
  • CEU camera interface: 16-bit on 224/176/169-pin, 8-bit on 144-pin, none on 100-pin. No capacitive touch and no LCD controller (see RA8D1 for graphics).
  • 1 MB SRAM = 896 KB user SRAM + 128 KB TCM. ADC: 2 x 12-bit units with up to 21 distinct inputs on 224-pin BGA and 20 on 176-pin LQFP.
  • Runs from 1.68-3.6 V and has an optional on-chip DC-DC regulator. Deep Software Standby (mode 3) draws about 1.6 uA typ in DCDC mode.
  • ROM boot modes: SCI (UART), USB and JTAG (Renesas Flash Programmer).
