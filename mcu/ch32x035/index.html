Design notes

  • USB is one full-speed controller (host OR device at a time). USB PD supports Source, Sink and DRP; 20-pin parts have USB device + PD only (no host).
  • Supply 2.0-5.5 V without USB/PD, 3.0-5.3 V when USB or PD is used; ADC needs >= 2.5 V. Internal 48 MHz oscillator, no crystal needed for USB.
  • Has a PIOC programmable protocol I/O co-processor (small 8-bit eMCU) for WS2812/1-wire style protocols; not comparable to RP2040 PIO.
  • No RTC. A 64-pin CH32X035R8T6 (60 GPIO, 14 ADC/touch) exists but is rarely stocked.
  • Factory bootloader over USB or UART: hold PC17 high at power-up. Debug is WCH 2-wire SDI via WCH-LinkE.
