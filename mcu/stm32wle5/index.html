Design notes

  • Needs a 32 MHz TCXO or crystal and RF matching; many designs use a pre-certified module (LoRa-E5, RAK3172) instead.
  • No USB: program over SWD or the UART/SPI ROM bootloader.
  • WLE5 includes the AES-256 accelerator; the cheaper WLE4 does not.
  • 2 USART + 1 LPUART, 3x I2C, 2x SPI (one with I2S), 1-channel DAC.
