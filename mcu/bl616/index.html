Design notes

  • BL616 (QFN40) has 19 GPIO; BL618 (QFN56) has 35 GPIO. Ai-M62 modules use BL616, Ai-M61 modules use BL618.
  • Rare combination of Wi-Fi 6 + BT 5.3 dual-mode (Classic + LE) + 802.15.4 on one radio; BL616C ordering code lists Thread, BL618M lists Zigbee/Thread.
  • USB 2.0 HS OTG with on-chip PHY; ROM can download over UART (GPIO21/22), USB or SDIO (hold GPIO2 high at reset).
  • SD/MMC host, Ethernet RMII MAC, DVP camera, DBI/QSPI/RGB display, MJPEG encoder and a mono audio codec on chip.
  • SRAM is 532 KB including caches and 4 KB HBN RAM (480 KB general-purpose OCRAM+WRAM); 2.97-3.63 V supply (datasheet lists the UARTs as 'support 5V IO').
  • Small ecosystem: no official Arduino/MicroPython; use the Bouffalo SDK or Zephyr (bl61x, Ai-M61/M62 kits, Sipeed M0S dock). External flash limit is a conservative estimate (Ai-Thinker modules support up to 16 MB).
