Apollo3 Blue

Ambiq, Apollo3. Ultra-low-power 48 MHz (96 MHz burst) Cortex-M4F with BLE 5.0, 1 MB flash and 384 KB SRAM at about 6 uA/MHz, suited to coin-cell wearables, voice and TinyML. Use the SparkFun Artemis module to avoid the BGA.

Design notes

  • The bare chip is a 5x5 mm, 0.5 mm-pitch 81-ball BGA (a 66-ball WLCSP also exists), so hand assembly is impractical. WRL-15484 is SparkFun's Artemis module (15.5 x 10.5 x 2.3 mm, 59 pads in two staggered 1.0 mm-pitch rows, FCC/IC/CE certified, 48 GPIO). Ambiq's 2023 brief also lists a QFN-64 (AMA3B1KK-KQR-B0), but stock is scarce.
  • 48 MHz is the nominal clock and 96 MHz is TurboSPOT burst mode. BLE 5.0 runs on a separate, dedicated controller core that is not user-programmable.
  • The six IOM masters are each I2C or SPI (6 total, shared), and there is an extra I2C/SPI slave (IOS). MSPI is a separate 1/2/4/8-bit SPI for external flash/PSRAM: XIP up to 64 MB and a memory-mapped PSRAM window of 16 MB.
  • The I2S port is slave-only and meant for PDM-microphone pass-through, not a general codec interface, so i2s is 0. Stereo PDM mic input is supported.
  • The ADC is 14-bit with 15 selectable inputs: 10 external single-ended pins (2 differential pairs) plus the temperature sensor and supply channels.
  • 8 CTIMERs give 16 independent PWM outputs. SparkFun lists 31 PWM-capable pins on Artemis.
  • The 6 uA/MHz is Ambiq's headline figure; CoreMark from flash at 48 MHz measures 10.3 uA/MHz. deepSleepUa is deep sleep at 3.3 V with 8 KB SRAM retained, XTAL/RTC on and BLE off (4.1 uA with all 384 KB retained).
  • The factory Secure Bootloader (SBL) supports UART wired updates at 115200 baud, selected by a boot pin configured in INFO0 (pin 47 on Artemis). SparkFun's SVL is a faster flash-resident bootloader installed on top. Secure boot plus an AES-128 engine count as crypto.
  • Arduino support is the SparkFun Arduino_Apollo3 core (v2, Mbed OS based; last release 2022, so effectively maintenance-only). Zephyr support is upstream (apollo3_evb).
