Design notes

  • No ROM bootloader: blank chips must be flashed once over SWD (e.g. to install an Arduino/UF2 bootloader); afterwards USB uploads work.
  • CAN-FD: 1 on E (32-pin), 2 on G/J/N. External CAN transceiver required.
  • uart/i2c/spi counts are SERCOMs: each SERCOM is either a USART, I2C or SPI, so the totals are shared (e.g. 6 SERCOMs = 3 UART + 2 I2C + 1 SPI). SERCOMs: 4/6/6/8 on E/G/J/N.
  • Two 12-bit ADCs (unique inputs 10/14/20/22 for E/G/J/N) plus a 16-bit sigma-delta ADC (1/2/3 differential channels).
  • No USB. 48 MHz up to 125 C; '64' ordering-suffix parts run 64 MHz up to 85 C.
  • No official KiCad symbol.
