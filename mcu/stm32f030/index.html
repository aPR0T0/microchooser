Design notes

  • Needs 2.4-3.6 V (higher minimum than most STM32s).
  • Peripheral count scales with memory: x4/x6 have 1 USART, 1 I2C, 1 SPI; x8 has 2/2/2; xC has 6 USART, 2 I2C, 2 SPI.
  • No USB, CAN, DAC or I2S; RTC without VBAT backup domain.
