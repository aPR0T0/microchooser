Design notes

  • All 3 FlexCAN controllers support CAN-FD.
  • 3 Ethernet MACs: 10/100 (IEEE1588), 1 GbE with AVB and 1 GbE with TSN; gigabit needs an RGMII PHY.
  • 2x USB 2.0 OTG with integrated high-speed PHYs; 2x uSDHC (SD 3.0 / eMMC 5.0).
  • Flashless: boots from FlexSPI NOR (two FlexSPI ports, HyperRAM/PSRAM capable); SEMC supports 8/16/32-bit SDRAM.
  • Display: eLCDIF + LCDIFv2 + MIPI-DSI (2-lane, PHY integrated), 2D vector GPU and PXP; camera: parallel CSI + MIPI-CSI2.
  • 2 MB SRAM total incl. 512 KB M7 TCM and 256 KB M4 TCM. Crypto: CAAM, PUF, OTFAD/IEE, HAB secure boot.
  • 289 MAPBGA (14x14 mm, 0.8 mm) only - realistically a 6-layer PCB. Consumer DVMAA runs the M7 at 1 GHz, industrial CVM8A at 800 MHz.
  • ADC channel count is conservative (2x 12-bit ADC); external-flash/PSRAM limits are approximate.
