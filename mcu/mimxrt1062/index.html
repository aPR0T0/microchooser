Design notes

  • 3 FlexCAN controllers but only CAN3 supports CAN-FD; CAN1/CAN2 are classic CAN 2.0B.
  • Flashless: boots/XIPs from external QSPI NOR on FlexSPI; FlexSPI2 can host QSPI PSRAM (Teensy 4.1 takes 2x 8 MB); SEMC adds SDRAM/parallel NOR/NAND.
  • 1 MB SRAM = 512 KB FlexRAM (configurable ITCM/DTCM/OCRAM) + 512 KB OCRAM.
  • 2x USB 2.0 OTG with integrated high-speed PHYs; 2x 10/100 Ethernet MACs (RMII, external PHY); 2x uSDHC (SD 3.0/eMMC 4.5).
  • BGA only: DVL/CVL = 10x10 mm 0.65 mm pitch, DVJ/CVJ = 12x12 mm 0.8 mm pitch - plan a 4+ layer PCB.
  • D-grade (consumer) runs 600 MHz; C-grade (industrial, e.g. CVL5B) is limited to 528 MHz. B-revision silicon replaces A.
  • ROM serial downloader over USB HID and UART. LCD (eLCDIF), parallel camera (CSI) and PXP 2D engine are included on RT1062 (not on RT1061).
