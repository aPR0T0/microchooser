XMC4700

Infineon, XMC4000. 144 MHz Cortex-M4F industrial MCU with 2 MB flash, 352 KB SRAM, Ethernet, USB OTG, SD/MMC, six classic CAN nodes and rich motor-control timers. Good for gateways, PLC-style I/O and drives.

Design notes

  • MultiCAN has 6 nodes and 256 message objects but is classic CAN 2.0B only (up to 1 Mbit/s). There is no CAN FD.
  • There are 6 USIC channels (3 modules x 2). Each one is UART, SPI (dual/quad), I2C, I2S or LIN, so the uart/i2c/spi/i2s counts all share these 6 channels.
  • USB is 2.0 full-speed OTG with an integrated PHY (no high-speed). The Ethernet 10/100 MAC uses MII/RMII on LQFP-144 and RMII only on LQFP-100. The XMC4800 is the same chip plus an EtherCAT slave controller.
  • The EBU external bus (SDRAM, NOR, SRAM; 16-bit muxed only on LQFP-100) can also drive 8080-style LCD modules. There is no QSPI flash controller; USIC quad-SPI is not memory-mapped.
  • pwmChannels counts 16 CCU4 slices plus 8 CCU8 slices x 2 compare channels (CCU8 also gives complementary outputs with dead time). There are also 2 POSIF encoder/hall interfaces and a 4-channel delta-sigma demodulator.
  • The LQFP-100 version has 24 ADC channels and 75 I/O. LQFP-144 has 32 ADC channels and 119 I/O, of which 26 (ports 14/15) are input-only analog/digital.
  • Both LQFP packages have an exposed die pad (7.0 x 7.0 mm incl. U-groove, 6.2 mm without) that must be soldered to VSS. The KiCad 1EP footprints listed are the closest library match; check them against Infineon's land pattern.
  • deepSleepUa is hibernate with the RTC running (VBAT 3.3 V), where SRAM is lost. Deep sleep with SRAM retention draws about 7 mA.
  • Supply is 3.3 V only (3.13-3.63 V).
  • ROM bootstrap loaders: ASC (UART) and CAN BSL, selected by the TCK/TMS levels at reset.
  • Arduino support is the XMC-for-Arduino core (KIT_XMC47_RELAX board); Zephyr has the xmc47_relax_kit board.
