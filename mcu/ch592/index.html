Design notes

  • BLE 5.4 (1M/2M PHY, +4.5 dBm, -95 dBm sensitivity). Needs a 32 MHz crystal; BLE stack is a closed-source WCH library.
  • Segment LCD driver up to 20x4 (CH592X). CH592A/CH591 are cut-down variants (no touch/I2C on CH591, 256 KB flash).
  • CH592D (QFN-20) has 12 GPIO and only 2 UARTs/4 ADC channels.
  • Factory USB/UART ISP bootloader; 2-wire debug (WCH-LinkE) must first be enabled via the ISP tool.
