Design notes

  • Serial: 1 USART (UART/SPI/I2S/IrDA/SmartCard) + 2 EUSART (UART/SPI) + 2 I2C. The UART and SPI counts share those 3 blocks.
  • IADC is 12-bit @ 1 Msps or 16-bit @ 76.9 ksps. Select OPNs (B310/B110/B120, 'ADC' pinout with 28 GPIO) add high-speed and high-accuracy (16-bit ENOB) modes. Any GPIO on ports A-D can be an analog input via ABUS.
  • 2 VDACs (2 channels each), 2 analog comparators, keypad scanner, and an MVP matrix-vector accelerator for TensorFlow Lite Micro.
  • TX power: B2x0/x20 parts reach +19.5 dBm; x10 parts +10 dBm. Bluetooth Channel Sounding needs a 40 MHz crystal and is limited to 10 dBm.
  • No USB and no ROM UART bootloader. Flash a blank chip over SWD (J-Link / Simplicity Link debugger); the Gecko Bootloader (UART/OTA) is optional and lives in flash.
  • Arduino core (Silicon Labs) supports XIAO MG24, SparkFun Thing Plus Matter (MGM240P), Nano Matter and the xG24 kits. CircuitPython supports the xG24 kits and Thing Plus Matter.
  • MGM240P module: pre-certified, crystal and antenna on board, 36 pads at 1.0 mm pitch. The PB32 version is +20 dBm.
