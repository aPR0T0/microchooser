Design notes

  • Peripherals vary a lot by package: F6/G6 have only USBD (device) and 1-2 UARTs; F8 has only USBFS; C8 and RB have both USB controllers, 4 UARTs, 2 I2C, 2 SPI.
  • 10 Mbit Ethernet MAC+PHY exists only on CH32V203RBT6.
  • Flash size in the part number is the zero-wait region; all parts have 224 KB total flash (the rest runs with wait states). RB can trade flash for up to 64 KB SRAM via option bytes.
  • CAN and USBD share a 512-byte buffer: both work together but USBD is then limited to 384 bytes.
  • Factory USB/UART ISP bootloader (BOOT0 pin). Debug is WCH 2-wire SDI via WCH-LinkE (not ARM SWD).
  • Runs 3.3 V only (2.4-3.6 V); most I/O are 5 V tolerant (FT).
