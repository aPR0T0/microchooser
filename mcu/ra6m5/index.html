Design notes

  • CAN: 2 channels. Part numbers starting R7FA6M5B... (feature set B, listed here) support CAN-FD on both channels. R7FA6M5A... parts are classic CAN only, so order the B parts if you need CAN-FD. External transceivers are required.
  • USBHS (480 Mbit/s, on-chip HS PHY, host/device) is only bonded out on the 176-pin LQFP/BGA and 100-pin BGA. The 144-pin and 100-pin LQFP parts have USBFS only.
  • Ethernet: one 10/100 MAC with an external PHY. The 176-pin and 100-pin LQFP are RMII-only, the 144-pin supports MII and RMII, and the 100-pin BGA has no Ethernet.
  • One SDHI/MMC host (4-bit SD, up to 8-bit eMMC).
  • 10 SCI channels (9 on the 100-pin BGA), each a UART, simple SPI or simple I2C, so they are shared. There are also 3 dedicated IIC and 2 SPI.
  • QSPI (64 MB memory-mapped window) and OSPI (128 MB CS0 + 256 MB CS1 windows, OctaFlash/OctaRAM) are both present.
  • Supply is 2.7-3.6 V only. ROM boot mode supports SCI (UART) and USB (Renesas Flash Programmer).
  • Used on the Arduino Portenta C33 (Arduino core, PlatformIO, MicroPython).
  • ADC: 2 x 12-bit units with up to 26 distinct inputs on 176-pin (22 on 144-pin, 17 on 100-pin). CTSU touch: 15 / 20 / 12 channels on 176 / 144 / 100-pin LQFP.
  • Deep Software Standby draws about 4.8 uA typ. Ordinary Software Standby is about 2.1 mA typ on this process. Active current is about 22 mA running CoreMark at 200 MHz.
  • A 1 MB (R7FA6M5BF...) version and BGA versions are also available.
