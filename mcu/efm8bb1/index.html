Design notes

  • Only 512 bytes of RAM (256 B internal + 256 B XRAM). ramKB shows 0 because the schema counts whole KB.
  • Programmed and debugged over Silicon Labs' 2-wire C2 interface (RSTb/C2CK + P2.0/C2D). You need a Silicon Labs USB Debug Adapter, a dev kit's on-board debugger or an open-source C2 programmer. Some EFM8 parts ship with a factory UART bootloader (AN945), but that isn't assumed here.
  • Peripherals: 1 UART, 1 SPI, 1 SMBus/I2C, 4 general 16-bit timers, a 3-channel PCA (PWM/capture), 2 comparators, 12-bit ADC (P0/P1 pins) and a temperature sensor.
  • Supply is 2.2-3.6 V only; the EFM8UB families add an on-chip 5 V regulator.
  • No Arduino/PlatformIO support. Use Keil C51 (free license through Simplicity Studio) or SDCC. Used in the Sonoff RF Bridge R2, so community firmware exists.
  • The 2 KB/4 KB (F2/F4) parts are cheaper; 'I' temperature-grade parts are rated to +125 °C.
