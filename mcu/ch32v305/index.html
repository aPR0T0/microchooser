Design notes

  • USB HS (480 Mbit/s) PHY is on-chip on every CH32V305. Only RB (LQFP-64) also has the full-speed USB host/device controller; FB/GB have USBHS only.
  • Package limits: FBP6 has 2 UARTs, 1 SPI, 1 CAN, no SDIO; GBU6 has 1 CAN and SDIO; RBT6 has 2 CAN and SDIO. No Ethernet (see CH32V307).
  • 128 KB zero-wait flash; total flash 480 KB on 256 KB-class parts (CH32V305CCT6, rarely stocked).
  • Debug is WCH 2-wire SDI via WCH-LinkE (not ARM SWD). Factory USB/UART ISP bootloader via BOOT0.
