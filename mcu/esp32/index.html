Design notes

  • No native USB: needs a USB-UART bridge (CP2102/CH340) plus the DTR/RTS auto-reset circuit for flashing.
  • Strapping pins GPIO0, 2, 5, 12 (MTDI selects flash voltage) and 15; GPIO34-39 are input-only; GPIO6-11 are wired to the SPI flash.
  • ADC2 channels cannot be used while Wi-Fi is active.
  • Bare chip needs external QSPI flash, a 40 MHz crystal and RF matching; most designs use the WROOM-32E module (3.0-3.6 V with its 3.3 V flash).
  • Only 4 MB of PSRAM is mapped at once; 8 MB needs the himem API. WROVER modules use GPIO16/17 for PSRAM.
  • Use chip revision v3 (D0WD-V3 / WROOM-32E); older revisions have errata and are not supported by esp-hal (Rust).
  • PWM count = 16 LED-PWM channels + 2 MCPWM units x 6 outputs.
