Design notes

  • 112 KB SRAM = 80 KB SRAM1 + 16 KB SRAM2 + 16 KB CCM SRAM.
  • 3 USART + 2 UART + 1 LPUART on 64-pin and larger; 48-pin has no UART4/5.
  • 3 x 12-bit ADCs (up to 36 channels on 100-pin); 2 external DAC channels; no HRTIM (see G474).
  • USB is full-speed device only.
