Design notes

  • No TrustZone on the H503 (unlike H56x/H57x); has HASH (SHA-2) and TRNG but no AES/PKA.
  • 3 USART + 1 LPUART; 2 I2C plus 2 I3C that share pins with the I2C buses.
  • Not a low-power part: Standby with RTC is about 3.3 uA and there is no Shutdown mode.
  • ROM bootloader supports USB DFU, FDCAN, USART, I2C, I3C and SPI.
