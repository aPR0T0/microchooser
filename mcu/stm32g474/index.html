Design notes

  • HRTIM: 6 x 16-bit counters, 184 ps resolution, 12 PWM outputs.
  • 128 KB SRAM = 80 KB SRAM1 + 16 KB SRAM2 + 32 KB CCM SRAM.
  • 5 x 12-bit ADCs (up to 42 channels on 100-pin); 7 DAC channels, 3 of them external outputs; 7 comparators, 6 op-amps.
  • 3 USART + 2 UART + 1 LPUART on 64-pin and larger (48-pin has no UARTs 4/5); 4th SPI and FMC (8080 LCD/SRAM) only on 80/100/128-pin parts.
  • USB is full-speed device only.
