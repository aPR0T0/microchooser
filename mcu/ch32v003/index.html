Design notes

  • No USB. Flash and debug use the 1-wire SWIO pin with a WCH-LinkE (~$4); ST-Link/J-Link do not work.
  • Has a factory UART bootloader (PD5/PD6, 115200 baud), but no BOOT pin: a blank chip boots straight into user flash, so you need a WCH-LinkE once to set START_MODE or flash an app that jumps to it.
  • Only 2 KB RAM and RV32EC (16 registers). ADC is 10-bit. One built-in op-amp/comparator.
  • ch32v003fun (bare-metal SDK) and rv003usb (bit-banged low-speed USB) are popular community projects.
  • Rated 2.7-5.5 V; I/O levels follow VDD, so it can talk 5 V logic when powered from 5 V.
