Design notes

  • No ROM bootloader: a blank chip must be programmed over SWD (FRDM-KL25Z uses its OpenSDA probe). KL27/KL17 add a ROM bootloader.
  • USB 2.0 full-speed OTG with on-chip transceiver and a 5 V-to-3.3 V regulator (VREGIN) that can power the chip from USB VBUS.
  • UART0 is a low-power UART (LPSCI) plus two standard UARTs; SPIs are 8-bit.
  • Also sold as 32/64 KB-flash MKL25Z32/MKL25Z64 variants.
  • Legacy Freescale-era part: LCSC stock is thin and qty-1 prices high; NXP's MCX A series is the modern replacement.
