Design notes

  • Up to 6 USART + 6 UART + 1 LPUART on 144/176-pin parts (5+5+1 on LQFP64); also 1x I3C.
  • Has HASH (SHA-2), PKA and TrustZone; the AES/SAES cipher engines are only on the STM32H573.
  • GPIO count is lower on SMPS (Q-suffix) parts, e.g. 110 instead of 112 on LQFP144.
  • USB is full-speed only (host + device) with a UCPD Type-C/PD controller; 1 Gbit Ethernet is not supported (10/100 MAC).
  • No LTDC; displays connect via the FMC (8080 parallel, not on LQFP64), SPI or DCMI/PSSI.
  • Some smaller-package ordering codes (H562) have fewer FDCAN/SDMMC instances; check the datasheet table.
