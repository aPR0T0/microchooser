Design notes

  • Serial sharing: SPIM0/TWIM0 and SPIM1/TWIM1 share hardware; SPIM2, SPIM3 (32 MHz) and both UARTEs are separate. Max is 4 SPI, or 2 I2C + 2 SPI + 2 UART at once.
  • USB 2.0 Full-Speed is device-only (no host).
  • VDDH accepts 2.5-5.5 V (e.g. USB VBUS or Li-ion). In that mode the REG0 output that feeds VDD/GPIO defaults to 1.8 V; set UICR REGOUT0 to get 3.3 V I/O.
  • The QFN40 package brings out only 18 GPIOs. The aQFN73 has 42 but needs fine-pitch PCB rules (via-in-pad/0.5 mm inner rows).
  • No ROM bootloader; flash over SWD. The micro:bit v2 uses an interface MCU for drag-and-drop flashing.
  • Not an official Matter target in nRF Connect SDK (use nRF52840/nRF5340/nRF54L15). MicroPython runs on the micro:bit v2 through the separate micro:bit port, not upstream ports/nrf.
  • Arduino support comes through community cores (e.g. arduino-nRF5 for the micro:bit v2).
