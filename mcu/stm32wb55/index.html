Design notes

  • Radio stack runs on the Cortex-M0+ and must be flashed as an encrypted ST binary via the FUS; it occupies part of the 1 MB flash/256 KB RAM.
  • Requires a 32 MHz HSE crystal; the embedded SMPS needs an external inductor.
  • UFQFPN48 (C) parts: 1 SPI, 10 ADC inputs, no touch sensing, 4x13 segment LCD; VFQFPN68 (R) parts: 2 SPI, 16 ADC inputs, 6 touch channels.
  • Single USART + LPUART; USB is full-speed device only (crystal-less); ROM bootloader supports USB DFU.
  • Datasheet lists Bluetooth 5.3; newer ST stacks add Bluetooth 5.4 features.
