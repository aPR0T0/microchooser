Design notes

  • RP2350A/RP2354A (QFN-60) have 30 GPIO and 4 ADC inputs; RP2350B/RP2354B (QFN-80) have 48 GPIO and 8 ADC inputs.
  • RP2350A/B need an external QSPI flash and a 12 MHz crystal; RP2354A/B have 2 MB flash stacked in the package (same pinout).
  • 3 PIO blocks (12 state machines) plus an HSTX block (e.g. DVI output); I2S, SD card and extra UARTs are done in PIO.
  • A second QSPI chip-select (on a GPIO) can add up to 16 MB of PSRAM or more flash.
  • Arm cores have a single-precision FPU and a double-precision coprocessor; the Hazard3 RISC-V cores have no FPU.
  • No calendar RTC (removed vs RP2040); the always-on timer keeps time in low-power states.
  • On-chip switching regulator takes 2.7-5.5 V on VREG_VIN, but IOVDD/VREG_AVDD still need 3.3 V.
  • Early A2 silicon has erratum RP2350-E9 (GPIO leakage, pull-downs unreliable); fixed from stepping A3, current stock is A4.
  • Deep-sleep figure is the P1.7 power-down state from the datasheet, including the external flash supply.
