Design notes

  • Blank chips need an ICSP programmer/debugger (PICkit 4/5, MPLAB Snap or ICD); there is no factory bootloader.
  • 32 MHz clock = 8 MIPS (4 clocks per instruction).
  • 1 EUSART; the 2 MSSP modules are each either I2C or SPI (i2c/spi counts are shared).
  • PWM = 4 CCP + 2 dedicated 10-bit PWM; also 2 CWG, 4 CLC, NCO, 24-bit SMT. DAC is only 5-bit.
  • Touch = capacitive voltage divider (CVD) sensing in hardware on the ADC2 inputs (mTouch library), not a dedicated touch controller.
  • One of the 18 I/O (MCLR/RA3) is input-only. The PIC16LF18446 variant runs 1.8-3.6 V.
