Design notes

  • Single USB OTG controller: full-speed with on-chip PHY; high-speed needs an external ULPI PHY.
  • RAM = 128 KB DTCM + 432 KB system RAM (192 KB shareable with ITCM) + 4 KB backup.
  • Two 16-bit ADCs plus one 12-bit ADC; 5 USART + 5 UART + 1 LPUART.
  • LQFP100 parts have only one usable Octo-SPI port (two on 144-pin) and 5 SPIs instead of 6.
  • No crypto accelerator (that is the STM32H733).
