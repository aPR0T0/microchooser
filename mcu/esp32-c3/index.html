Design notes

  • Native USB is a fixed-function Serial/JTAG controller (CDC-ACM + JTAG): great for flashing/debugging, but no HID/MSC or custom USB devices.
  • Strapping pins GPIO2, GPIO8 and GPIO9; GPIO18/19 are the USB D-/D+ pins.
  • Bare ESP32-C3 needs external QSPI flash; all variants need a 40 MHz crystal and RF matching.
  • Espressif now recommends the 'X' (chip v1.1) parts for new designs: ESP32-C3FH4X (only 16 GPIO) / FH8X and ESP32-C3-MINI-1-N4X/H4X (the plain MINI-1-N4 is NRND).
  • ADC2 is unreliable on early revisions; use ADC1 channels (GPIO0-4).
