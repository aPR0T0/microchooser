Design notes

  • Serial sharing: SPIM0/TWIM0 and SPIM1/TWIM1 share hardware; SPIM2, SPIM3 (32 MHz) and both UARTEs are separate. Max is 4 SPI, or 2 I2C + 2 SPI + 2 UART at once.
  • USB 2.0 Full-Speed is device-only (no host).
  • No ROM bootloader. Most boards (Adafruit, Seeed XIAO, nice!nano) ship the Adafruit UF2 bootloader in flash, but a blank chip or bare module needs SWD.
  • VDDH accepts 2.5-5.5 V (USB VBUS or Li-ion directly). In that mode GPIO voltage comes from REG0, which defaults to 1.8 V; set UICR REGOUT0 for 3.3 V I/O.
  • The bare chip is aQFN73 only (0.5 mm pitch with inner rows, needs via-in-pad or fine-pitch PCB). Most hobby designs use a pre-certified module such as Raytac MDBT50Q or Ebyte E73-2G4M08S1C.
  • The QSPI XIP window is 128 MB; 16 MB is a conservative practical figure. Mainline MicroPython, CircuitPython and Arduino (Adafruit nRF52 core / Arduino mbed) all support it.
