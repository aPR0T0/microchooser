Design notes

  • 4 SCI channels (SCI0/1/2/9; 3 on packages of 36 pins or fewer). Each one works as a UART, simple SPI or simple I2C (master-only), so UART, SPI and I2C share them. On top of that there is 1 dedicated IIC and 1 dedicated SPI.
  • ROM SCI boot mode: hold MD (P201) low at reset and flash over a UART with Renesas Flash Programmer. There is no USB.
  • Runs directly from 1.6-5.5 V and needs no crystal (HOCO up to 64 MHz, trimmed). No USB, CAN, DAC or SD host.
  • CTSU2 capacitive touch: 30 channels on 64-pin, 20 on 48-pin and 11 on 32-pin parts. The 12-bit ADC has 13 inputs (10 on 32-pin).
  • Timers: 1 x 32-bit + 6 x 16-bit GPT (14 PWM outputs on 48/64-pin) and 2 low-power AGT. AES-128/256 and TRNG on chip.
  • Not supported by upstream Zephyr (only RA2A1/RA2L1 are). Use Renesas FSP / e2 studio.
  • A 14 x 14 mm 0.8 mm-pitch LQFP-64 (CFK) is also available.
