Design notes

  • Two 10-bit ADCs (ADC0: 12 inputs, ADC1: 8 inputs, sharing pins); the PTC takes over ADC0 while touch is active.
  • Three 8-bit DACs, but only DAC0 has a pin output (the others feed the analog comparators).
  • PTC: 12 self-capacitance or 36 mutual-capacitance touch channels (QTouch library).
  • The SOIC-20 is the 300-mil wide-body package.
  • Blank chips are flashed over the 1-wire UPDI pin; a USB-serial adapter plus a resistor (SerialUPDI) or any cheap UPDI programmer works.
