Design notes

  • 192 KB RAM includes 64 KB CCM RAM (CPU-only, not DMA accessible).
  • USB OTG_HS runs at full speed with its internal PHY; 480 Mbit/s needs an external ULPI PHY.
  • 4 USART + 2 UART; classic bxCAN (not CAN-FD); no Ethernet or camera (see F407).
  • FSMC (parallel 8080 LCD / SRAM / NOR) only on 100-pin and larger packages.
  • No crypto accelerator (only RNG); the STM32F415 adds AES/HASH.
