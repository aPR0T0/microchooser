Design notes

  • UCPD provides Type-C CC handling/PD PHY only; there is no USB data controller (use G0B1 or C071 for USB).
  • Standard-pinout 28/32-pin parts expose only one UCPD port with one CC line; N-suffix parts have full dual UCPD but lose a GPIO.
  • 4 USART + 1 LPUART, 2 I2C, 2 SPI (one with I2S), HDMI-CEC; no CAN.
  • 36 KB SRAM without parity (32 KB with parity).
