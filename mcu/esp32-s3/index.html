Design notes

  • USB OTG (FS) and the USB Serial/JTAG controller share one internal PHY, so only one is on the USB pins at a time.
  • Octal PSRAM/flash (S3R8, -R8/-R16 modules) takes GPIO33-37; on WROOM-1 N16R8 IO35-37 are unavailable (33 usable GPIO).
  • Strapping pins GPIO0, GPIO3, GPIO45 and GPIO46; bare chips need a 40 MHz crystal and RF matching.
  • S3R8 octal-PSRAM parts are rated only to 65 °C ambient (85 °C with PSRAM ECC enabled).
  • Datasheet quotes up to 1 GB external memory space; 32 MB octal-flash modules exist, PSRAM up to 16 MB in-package.
  • PWM count = 8 LED-PWM channels + 2 MCPWM units x 6 outputs.
