Design notes

  • USB FS device runs crystal-less from HSI48 + CRS; ROM bootloader supports USB DFU.
  • 4 USART, 2 I2C, 2 SPI (both I2S-capable), classic bxCAN, HDMI-CEC, 2 comparators.
  • USB and CAN share a 1 KB packet SRAM (768 B left for USB when CAN is clocked).
