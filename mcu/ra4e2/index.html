Design notes

  • One CANFD channel (classic CAN and CAN-FD, ISO 11898-1). It needs an external transceiver.
  • USBFS is device-only (no host) and is missing on the 32-pin QFN. ROM boot mode supports SCI (UART), USB and SWD.
  • Only 2 SCI channels (SCI0, SCI9), each a UART, simple SPI or simple I2C. There is no classic IIC block: I2C is done by the single I3C module, which also talks to legacy I2C devices and is counted as i2c here. There are also 2 dedicated SPI.
  • Supply is 2.7-3.6 V only (no 5 V operation), though some pins are 5 V tolerant.
  • No capacitive touch, QSPI or crypto accelerator (TrustZone + TRNG only). There is 1 DAC channel and the 12-bit ADC has 12 inputs on 64-pin parts.
  • Deep Software Standby draws about 4.2 uA typ (about 16 uA when keeping 1 KB standby SRAM). Plain Software Standby is about 1.4 mA on this process. Active current is about 8.2 mA running CoreMark at 100 MHz.
  • The QFN-32 exposed pad is 3.2 x 3.2 mm, and KiCad has no exact match.
