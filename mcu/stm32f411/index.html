Design notes

  • All 5 SPIs can act as I2S (2 full-duplex), and SDIO is available even on the 48-pin package.
  • No CAN, no DAC, no Ethernet; 3 USART, 3 I2C.
  • USB OTG FS needs an HSE crystal for reliable USB timing.
