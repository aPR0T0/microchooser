Design notes

  • No Wi-Fi/Bluetooth: pair with an ESP32-C6/C5 over SDIO (ESP-Hosted) for wireless.
  • Requires external QSPI flash (up to 64 MB) and a 40 MHz crystal; PSRAM is 1.8 V in-package.
  • USB: one High-Speed OTG with internal PHY plus one Full-Speed OTG; a separate USB Serial/JTAG controller is also available.
  • Counts include LP peripherals (LP UART, LP I2C, LP SPI); I3C and the analog I2C are not counted. Three classic CAN 2.0 (TWAI) controllers.
  • Use the 'X' parts (chip revision v3.x, 400 MHz); earlier v1.x silicon (no X suffix) is slower and unsupported by esp-hal. Datasheet is still pre-release.
  • No KiCad library footprint for the QFN-104 (0.35 mm pitch, 7.5 mm EP).
