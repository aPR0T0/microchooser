Design notes

  • USB 2.0 FS device and host; crystal-less operation (HSI48 + clock recovery) works in device mode only.
  • ROM bootloader supports USB DFU plus USART/I2C/SPI.
  • Only 2 USARTs and no CAN; consider the C092 for FDCAN or G0B1 for more UARTs.
  • Part numbers ending in N have a VDDIO2 pin (one fewer GPIO).
