Design notes

  • 144 KB SRAM without parity, 128 KB with parity enabled.
  • 6 USART + 2 LPUART, 3 I2C, 3 SPI (2 with I2S), 2 FDCAN, 2 UCPD (only 1 port on standard-pinout 32-pin parts).
  • No HSE crystal oscillator on 32-pin packages; USB then runs crystal-less from HSI48 + CRS.
  • ROM bootloader supports USB DFU and FDCAN as well as USART/I2C/SPI.
  • Popular in 3D-printer CAN toolhead boards (Klipper) and USB-C PD sinks.
