Design notes

  • Needs an external QSPI flash chip (e.g. W25Q16) and a 12 MHz crystal.
  • SD card, I2S, extra UARTs, WS2812 and even CAN can be done in PIO.
  • Certified for 200 MHz operation since 2024 (SDK 2.1+).
