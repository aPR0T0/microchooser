Design notes

  • CAN: 4x CAN-FD controllers (up to 8 Mbit/s). These are HPMicro's own CAN IP, not the Bosch M_CAN used on HPM5300/HPM6200, so the drivers differ. The HPM6730 is the CAN 2.0-only sibling.
  • 2x USB 2.0 High-Speed OTG, each with its own integrated HS PHY.
  • Flashless: boots from external serial NOR on XPI0/XPI1 (Quad/Octal SPI, HyperFlash; 255 MB memory-mapped window per XPI). The HPM6754 is the variant with 4 MB in-package flash.
  • External RAM: the FEMC SDRAM controller (8/16/32-bit, 166 MHz, 256 MB window) is the usual choice. The XPIs also support Quad/Octal SPI PSRAM and HyperRAM; psramMaxMB 64 assumes the largest common OPI PSRAM/HyperRAM part.
  • Ethernet: 2x 10/100/1000 MACs with RGMII/RMII (external PHY needed), IEEE 1588 timestamps.
  • The BGA-196 (IAN) is cut down: 125 GPIO, 18 analog inputs, 1 SD/eMMC (SDXC0), 16-bit SDRAM only, ENET1 limited to 100 Mbit, and XPI0 brought out only on its CB port (boot flash must go there).
  • ROM serial boot/ISP over UART0 or USB0 (USB-HID), selected by BOOT_MODE pins PZ06/PZ07. Debug is JTAG.
  • Single 3.0-3.6 V supply; on-chip DCDC makes the 1.275 V core rail. IO banks run at 3.3 V or 1.8 V. Separate VBAT (2.4-3.6 V) for the RTC/battery domain draws about 5.5 uA in shutdown.
  • Power: about 128 mA from 3.3 V at 816 MHz on one core with peripherals off, and about 330 mA with both cores and all peripherals on. Plan for heat.
  • Software: HPM SDK (GCC/CMake, free SEGGER Embedded Studio) and RT-Thread BSPs. Not in upstream Zephyr (HPMicro maintains its own fork). The hpm-hal Rust crate (embassy) exists but is marked work-in-progress.
