Design notes

  • No ROM bootloader: blank chips must be flashed once over SWD (e.g. to install an Arduino/UF2 bootloader); afterwards USB uploads work.
  • ramKB = 32 KB SRAM + 8 KB low-power SRAM.
  • Backup mode 0.64 uA with RTC; Standby with RTC and full RAM retention ~1.4 uA. ~40 uA/MHz needs the internal buck regulator (77-95 uA/MHz on LDO).
  • uart/i2c/spi counts are SERCOMs: each SERCOM is either a USART, I2C or SPI, so the totals are shared (e.g. 6 SERCOMs = 3 UART + 2 I2C + 1 SPI). 6 SERCOMs on all packages (one sits in the low-power domain).
  • 3 op-amps, 2 comparators, AES-256 and TRNG. ADC inputs 10/14/20 and PTC self-cap channels 7/10/16 for E/G/J.
  • Order revision B parts (...B-...); revision A was engineering samples only.
