Design notes

  • 10 LP_FLEXCOMM blocks, each configurable as LPUART, LPSPI or LPI2C (or LPUART+LPI2C together): uart/i2c/spi counts are per type, not simultaneous (10 blocks total). Plus 2x I3C.
  • Both FlexCAN controllers support CAN-FD.
  • 100-pin HLQFP (VKLT) has NO high-speed USB, uSDHC, capacitive touch or smart-card interface and only 78 GPIO; use VFBGA-184 or HDQFP-172 for the full feature set.
  • USB: one high-speed host/device with on-chip HS PHY + one full-speed host/device with on-chip PHY. Ethernet is 10/100 MAC (RMII/MII, external PHY).
  • Secondary Cortex-M33 core lacks the FPU/TrustZone/MPU of the primary core. eIQ Neutron NPU, PowerQuad DSP, EdgeLock secure enclave.
  • No dedicated LCD or camera controller: FlexIO / SmartDMA drive 8080 displays and parallel cameras in the SDK.
  • FlexSPI (octal/quad NOR, HyperRAM/PSRAM, XIP); external flash/PSRAM limits here are approximate.
  • HDQFP-172 uses two staggered rows of 0.65 mm-pitch leads. ROM ISP includes USB and UART.
