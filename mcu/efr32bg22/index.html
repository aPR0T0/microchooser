Design notes

  • Bluetooth LE only (no 802.15.4), so no Thread/Zigbee/Matter. The EFR32MG22/MG24 are the 802.15.4 siblings.
  • Serial: 2 USART (UART/SPI/I2S) + 1 low-energy EUART (UART only, works in EM2) + 2 I2C. PDM microphone input; no DAC. Max TX power is about +6 dBm.
  • Sleep figure is EM2 with RAM retention and RTC. Active current is about 27 µA/MHz.
  • No USB and no ROM UART bootloader. Flash over SWD; the Gecko Bootloader (UART/OTA) is optional and lives in flash.
  • BGM220P is the pre-certified module (BGM220 Explorer Kit, supported by the Silicon Labs Arduino core). The module pad pitch here is approximate; use Silicon Labs' land pattern.
  • Exposed-pad sizes aren't listed because the Silicon Labs package drawing couldn't be confirmed.
