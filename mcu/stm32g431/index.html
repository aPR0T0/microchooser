Design notes

  • 32 KB SRAM = 16 KB SRAM1 + 6 KB SRAM2 + 10 KB CCM SRAM.
  • No Quad-SPI and no FMC on the G431 (G474/G491 have Quad-SPI).
  • UART counts depend on package: 3 USART + UART4 + LPUART on 64-pin and larger, 3 USART + LPUART on 48-pin, 2 USART + LPUART on 32-pin.
  • 2 ADCs (up to 23 channels), 4 DAC channels of which 2 are external outputs, 4 comparators, 3 op-amps.
  • USB is full-speed device only; crystal-less USB is only rated from -15 to 85 C.
