Design notes

  • No ROM bootloader and no MD/boot-mode pin: a blank chip can only be flashed over SWD (J-Link, E2 Lite, or the J-Link on the FPB-RA0E1 board). Field updates over UART need your own bootloader.
  • Serial ports come from a 6-channel Serial Array Unit (SAU): up to 3 UARTs (one LIN-capable), 3 simplified SPI or 3 simplified (master-only) I2C, one function per channel. There is also one UARTA and one IICA. uart = 3 SAU UARTs + UARTA; spi = the 3 SAU simplified-SPI channels (there is no dedicated SPI block), so UART and SPI counts compete for the same SAU channels.
  • Runs directly from 1.6-5.5 V, so a 5 V supply is fine; HOCO runs at 24/32 MHz with no crystal needed.
  • No USB, CAN, DAC, capacitive touch or crypto engine (TRNG only). The 12-bit ADC has 10 inputs on 32-pin parts and 6 on the 20-pin package.
  • Timer Array Unit: 8 x 16-bit channels (up to 7 PWM outputs) plus a 32-bit interval timer.
  • 32 KB flash versions (R7FA0E1053...) also exist. The TSSOP-20 (CSD) comes in 64 KB only; an LSSOP-20 (CSC) is also offered.
  • The QFN-32 exposed pad is 3.2 x 3.2 mm. KiCad has 3.1 and 3.3 mm variants but no exact match.
