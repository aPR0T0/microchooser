Design notes

  • 2 USART + 1 LPUART, 2 I2C, 2 SPI (one with I2S), 2 LPTIM; no USB/CAN/DAC.
  • SO-8 (J) version has only 6 I/Os.
  • ROM bootloader is USART/I2C only.
  • Internal 16 MHz RC + PLL reaches 64 MHz without a crystal.
