Design notes

  • 16 MHz requires 4.5-5.5 V (8 MHz from 2.7 V); the ATmega2560V runs from 1.8 V at lower clocks.
  • Blank chips need an ISP programmer to install the Arduino (stk500v2) bootloader.
  • Only offered in TQFP-100 (0.5 mm pitch) and BGA: not breadboard-friendly; relatively expensive for an 8-bit MCU.
  • RTC = asynchronous Timer2 with a 32.768 kHz crystal on TOSC pins.
