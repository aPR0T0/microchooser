Design notes

  • Cortex-M4 without FPU. No USB, no CAN. I2S is half-duplex and shares pins with SPI.
  • TSSOP-20 parts have 1 USART + 1 UART and 1 SPI; x8 = 64 KB flash / 16 KB SRAM, x4 = 16 KB / 8 KB.
  • ROM bootloader on USART1/USART2 via BOOT0; standard Arm SWD probes work. Use Artery's firmware library (not register-compatible with STM32).
