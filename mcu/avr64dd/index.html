AVR64DD

Microchip, AVR DD. Low-cost 5 V AVR from 14 to 32 pins with multi-voltage I/O, 12-bit ADC and DAC; a modern, cheap replacement for ATmega328-class designs.

Design notes

  • MVIO: PORTC can run from a separate VDDIO2 rail (1.8-5.5 V) for level shifting.
  • ADC inputs scale with pins: 7/13/19/23 for 14/20/28/32 pins. GPIO counts include the input-only PF6/RESET pin.
  • The 14/20-pin parts are covered by a separate data sheet (AVR64DD14/20).
  • No official KiCad symbol yet.
  • Blank chips are flashed over the 1-wire UPDI pin; a USB-serial adapter plus a resistor (SerialUPDI) or any cheap UPDI programmer works.
  • Arduino support via the community DxCore.
