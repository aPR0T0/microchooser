Design notes

  • R7FA4M1AB3CFM (LQFP-64) is the MCU on the Arduino Uno R4 Minima and WiFi.
  • Runs directly from 5 V, and USB still works then: feed 3.8-5.5 V to VCC_USB_LDO and the on-chip regulator makes the 3.3 V USB supply.
  • CAN is one classic CAN 2.0B controller (no CAN-FD) and needs an external transceiver.
  • 4 SCI channels (SCI0/1/2/9). Each works as a UART, simple SPI or simple I2C, so they are shared. There are also 2 dedicated IIC and 2 dedicated SPI (1 SPI on the 40-pin QFN).
  • ROM boot mode, selected with the MD pin, supports both SCI (UART) and USB through Renesas Flash Programmer (a Renesas protocol, not DFU). Arduino boards add their own USB bootloader (double-tap reset).
  • SSIE (I2S) is only on 100-pin packages. The segment LCD controller is on 64/100-pin parts. The 14-bit ADC has 25 inputs on 100-pin, 18 on 64-pin and 14 on 48-pin.
  • One 12-bit DAC output. The two 8-bit DACs only feed the low-power comparators. There are also 4 op-amps.
  • No SDHI/SDIO and no QSPI: use SPI mode for microSD.
  • The QFN-48 exists in two drawings (PWQN0048KB-A with a 5.5 mm EP, PWQN0048KC-A with 5.3 mm). A 5.3 mm land suits both.
  • activeUaPerMhz uses CoreMark from flash (16.4 mA at 48 MHz). A while(1) loop draws about half that.
