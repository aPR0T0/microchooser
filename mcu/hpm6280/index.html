Design notes

  • CAN: 4x Bosch M_CAN (MCAN) controllers, all CAN-FD (up to 8 Mbit/s), plus 4x LIN. All four are available on every package.
  • 1x USB 2.0 High-Speed OTG with integrated HS PHY.
  • No SD/SDIO host, Ethernet or I2S: use SPI for microSD.
  • Flashless: boots from external serial NOR on the single XPI0 (256 MB window; Quad/Octal SPI). The HPM6284 is the variant with 4 MB in-package flash. The XPI also supports PSRAM/HyperRAM, but it shares the one controller with the boot flash, so psramMaxMB is left at 0.
  • The '-PA' package is an eLQFP-144: its bottom exposed pad is VSS and must be soldered. The datasheet gives two lead-frame options (EP 5.0 mm or 9.74 mm); 5 x 5 mm is listed here as the safe land size. Confirm with HPMicro before layout.
  • Motor/power control: 2x 8-ch high-resolution PWM (100 ps), 2x 8-ch PWM (3 ns), 4x QEI/Hall, 2 programmable logic arrays (PLA) and a sigma-delta filter (SDM). DAC outputs are only on the LQFP-144; the BGA-116 and eLQFP-100 have 73 GPIO and 15 analog inputs.
  • ROM serial boot/ISP over UART0 or USB0 (USB-HID), selected by BOOT_MODE pins PA20/PA21. Debug is JTAG.
  • Single 3.0-3.6 V supply; on-chip DCDC makes the core rail. IO banks run at 3.3 V or 1.8 V. VBAT (2.4-3.6 V) powers the RTC domain at about 1.6 uA.
  • Power: about 84 mA from 3.3 V at 600 MHz on one core with peripherals off, and about 155 mA on both cores. Stop mode still draws a few mA.
  • Software: HPM SDK (GCC/CMake, free SEGGER Embedded Studio) and RT-Thread BSPs. Not in upstream Zephyr (HPMicro maintains its own fork). The hpm-hal Rust crate (embassy) exists but is marked work-in-progress.
