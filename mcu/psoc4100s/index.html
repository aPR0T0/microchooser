PSoC 4100S / 4100S Plus

Infineon, PSoC 4. 48 MHz Cortex-M0+ with best-in-class CapSense touch, opamps and a 12-bit SAR ADC, running from 1.71-5.5 V. The 4100S Plus adds 128 KB flash, 5 serial blocks and classic CAN.

Design notes

  • Serial ports come from SCBs that are each I2C, SPI or UART at runtime: 5 on the 4100S Plus, 3 on the 4100S. The uart/i2c/spi counts are per-type maxima and share the same blocks.
  • CAN is classic CAN 2.0B (no FD) and only on 4100S Plus parts with a 6 or 7 in the suffix (e.g. -S465, -S475). CapSense needs a 5 or 7 there (e.g. -S455, -S475), and plain 4100S parts with -S43x have CapSense.
  • Runs directly from 1.71-5.5 V, so 5 V systems need no regulator.
  • There is no hardware calendar RTC. The WCO 32 kHz crystal plus watchdog counters are used for timekeeping in firmware.
  • The two 7-bit IDACs are current DACs, mainly for CapSense, and there is no voltage DAC. Also on chip: 2 opamps (CTBm), 2 low-power comparators and Smart I/O logic (no UDBs).
  • The 4100S Plus has a TRNG but no crypto accelerator.
  • There is no ROM bootloader, so blank chips are programmed over SWD (KitProg3/MiniProg4). A bootloader can be added in firmware.
  • CapSense works on any GPIO except the CMOD capacitor pin, so touchChannels is the GPIO count minus one.
  • The SAR ADC sequencer has 16 single-ended channels via the AMUX bus. The 4100S (non-Plus) has 64 KB flash, 8 KB SRAM, 5 TCPWMs and up to 36 GPIO.
  • The larger PSoC 4100S Plus 256 KB (CY8C4148) and 4100S Max (CY8C4149) are separate lines and are not included here.
