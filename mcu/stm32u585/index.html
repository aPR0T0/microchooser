Design notes

  • Adds AES, SAES (side-channel resistant), PKA and OTFDEC over the U575; otherwise identical peripherals.
  • One 14-bit ADC + one 12-bit ADC; 3 USART + 2 UART + 1 LPUART; no I2S (use SAI).
  • Targets PSA Certified Level 3 / SESIP3 designs; used on the B-U585I-IOT02A Discovery kit.
  • Q-suffix part numbers include the SMPS step-down converter (needs an external inductor).
