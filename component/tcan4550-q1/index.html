Design notes

  • Pin-compatible with the TCAN4550, with wider VSUP (5.5-30 V), ±58 V bus and 8 Mbit/s
  • VCCOUT 5 V LDO can supply up to 70 mA to external loads
  • Needs a 20 or 40 MHz crystal or clock; same tINACTIVE fail-safe as the TCAN4550
  • Costs about the same as the industrial TCAN4550 at LCSC
