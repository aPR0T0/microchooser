Design notes

  • dToF (not triangulation): 0.05–12 m on 80% reflectivity, but only 4 m on 10% (dark) targets. Systematic error is ±20 mm across the range
  • Scan rate is 6–12 Hz and defaults to 6 Hz (0.54° step at 4000 samples/s). Speed is set by UART command. There is no M_CTR/PWM pin
  • Two-way UART (Tx and Rx): 230400 baud 8N1 with 3.3 V logic. Supply is 5 V (4.8–5.2 V)
  • Current is 340 mA typical and 480 mA max, with an 840 mA–1 A start-up peak and 45 mA max in sleep (motor stopped). YDLIDAR recommends a 5 V 1 A supply
  • Ambient light is rated to 60 klux (reference only). Don't point the window straight at the sun
  • USB comes from the included adapter board (USB-C). The sensor itself is UART-only
