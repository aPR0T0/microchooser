CA-IF1042LVS

Chipanalog communication IC. Low-cost Chipanalog CAN FD transceiver (5 Mbit/s), pin-compatible with the TCAN1042V, with VIO for 3.3 V MCUs and standby with remote wake. A budget second source for CAN FD motor-controller buses.

Design notes

  • 'LVS' = ±42 V family, VIO on pin 5, SOIC-8; CA-IF1042LS has NC on pin 5
  • 5 V VCC; VIO 3.0-5.5 V; STB high = standby with remote wake
  • Bus fault only ±42 V (±6 kV HBM), lower than the TI/NXP parts
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
