ADIN1110

Analog Devices communication IC. 10BASE-T1L single-pair Ethernet MAC-PHY on SPI (IEEE 802.3cg): 10 Mbit/s over one twisted pair up to 1.7 km. For long tethers and remote sensors on field robots, from MCUs without an Ethernet MAC.

Design notes

  • SPI up to 25 MHz, OPEN Alliance or generic protocol; 16 MAC filters, 28 KB buffer, IEEE 1588 timestamps
  • Runs from one 1.8 V or 3.3 V rail; the 2.4 Vpp long-reach mode needs AVDD_H = 3.3 V
  • VDDIO 1.8, 2.5 or 3.3 V for the SPI pins
  • Needs a 25 MHz crystal and external MDI termination resistors
  • Power-down modes do not wake on line energy
  • Mainline Linux driver (adin1110)
