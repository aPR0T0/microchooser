Design notes

  • Ethernet/USB to a computer; no MCU device mapping. The default output is ENET UDP (L2 at 192.168.1.62, sending from port 6101 to 192.168.1.2:6201). The alternative is 3.3 V TTL UART at 4,000,000 baud through the included USB Type-C adapter, but only one output can be active at a time
  • Range is 30 m @90% reflectivity and 15 m @10%. Near blind zone is 0.05 m (reference only)
  • FOV is 360°×90° in normal mode and 360°×96° in NEGA mode (the factory default). Range is slightly shorter in the extra 6°. A 2D mode gives a 180°/192° line at the same 64,000 pts/s
  • rateHz is the 5.55 Hz horizontal rotation and zones = 64,000 effective pts/s ÷ 5.55 Hz. Raw sampling is 128,000 pts/s and vertical scan is 216 Hz. Angular resolution is 0.64° and distance resolution 4.5 mm
  • Built-in IMU (3-axis accelerometer + 3-axis gyro), sampled at 1 kHz and reported at 500 Hz. It is disabled by default
  • Power is 10 W at 25 °C on 12 V DC (≈830 mA), up to 13 W in self-heating mode when cold. No point cloud is output until it warms up
  • Rated to 100 klux strong light, IP54, -10 to 50 °C, 905 nm Class 1
