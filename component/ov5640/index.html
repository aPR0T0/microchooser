Design notes

  • Max frame rates: 2592×1944 15 fps, 1080p 30 fps, 720p 60 fps, VGA 90 fps, QVGA 120 fps.
  • Autofocus only works on modules with a VCM lens. The AF firmware is loaded over I²C at start-up.
  • Runs warm, so give it heat-sinking (the Adafruit breakout has a thermal pad). OmniVision recommends MIPI for high-resolution YUV.
  • Full 5 MP frames on an ESP32-S3 need PSRAM even with JPEG.
