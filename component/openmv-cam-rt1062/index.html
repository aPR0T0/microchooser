Design notes

  • A full camera computer, not just a sensor. It runs your vision code and talks to the robot MCU over UART, SPI, I²C or CAN (1 Mb/s).
  • Image pipelines run at about 40 fps at QVGA and below. Full 5 MP stills are possible.
  • Takes 4.7–5.7 V on VIN and draws about 130 mA active. I/O is 3.3 V and not 5 V tolerant. Swappable sensor modules include global-shutter and thermal.
