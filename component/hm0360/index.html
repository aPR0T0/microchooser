Himax HM0360 low-power VGA mono camera

Himax camera. An ultra-low-power BSI monochrome VGA sensor for always-on TinyML vision (person detection, wake-on-motion). Draws about 8 mA at VGA 60 fps and a few hundred µA in monitor mode. Runs on an RP2040, Arduino or Cortex-M.

Design notes

  • Despite the 'SPI camera' name, the Arducam board streams 1-, 4- or 8-bit parallel/serial data, not SPI.
  • Himax's own FPC module HM0360-MWA-00FW703 (30-pin, 85° diagonal lens) is on LCSC as C17291166.
  • The array is 656×496, windowed to 640×480. Has binning and ROI readout and context switching for low-power duty cycling.
