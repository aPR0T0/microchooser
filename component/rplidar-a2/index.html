Design notes

  • Laser triangulation, 785 nm, Class 1; indoor use or outdoors without direct sunlight.
  • White target 12 m, black target 10 m, minimum 0.2 m (Enhanced mode).
  • UART 256000 baud 8N1, 3.3 V TTL (RX is current-driven, ≥1.6 V). USB only via the separate adapter board in the kit.
  • MOTOCTL pin: 25 kHz PWM sets speed (~60% duty ≈ 10 Hz), DC high = full speed, low = stop. Scan rate 5–15 Hz.
  • 5 V (4.9–5.2 V): 450 mA typ, 600 mA max with motor running; up to 2.5 A inrush and 1.5 A at start-up.
  • 16 kHz mode needs the extended high-speed protocol (current rplidar_sdk / rplidar_ros).
  • Accuracy is from Slamtec's A2 spec page (shared across A2 models); the A2M12 datasheet itself gives no accuracy figure.
