Design notes

  • Max UART baud 3 Mbaud
  • supplyV is VREGIN (3.0-5.25 V) through the internal regulator; or tie VREGIN to VDD and feed 3.0-3.6 V
  • Only the QFN-24 has a VIO pin; QFN-20/28 I/O runs at VDD
  • A GPIO can be set up as an RS-485 driver enable
  • Configure VID/PID, strings and GPIO with Silicon Labs' Simplicity Xpress Configurator
