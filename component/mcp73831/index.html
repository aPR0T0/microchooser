Design notes

  • No battery protection: use a protected cell or add DW01A + FS8205A.
  • STAT is tri-state (high/low/Hi-Z), so it can drive two LEDs or one MCU pin.
  • Charge current = 1000 V / RPROG; keep it ≤ 1C of the cell.
  • SOT-23 heats up at 500 mA from 5 V: give it copper.
