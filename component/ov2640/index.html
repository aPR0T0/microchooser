Design notes

  • On-chip JPEG makes it usable on an ESP32 without PSRAM-heavy processing; raw RGB565 above QVGA needs PSRAM.
  • Max frame rates: UXGA 15 fps, SVGA 30 fps, CIF 60 fps.
  • 24-pin 0.5 mm FPC pinouts differ between vendors. Check the module against your board's socket (ESP32-CAM style is the most common).
  • Seeed has discontinued its OV2640 module in favour of the OV3660/OV5640. For new designs, consider those or the GC2145.
