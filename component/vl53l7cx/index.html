VL53L7CX

STMicroelectronics distance sensor. Wide-angle 8x8-zone ToF sensor (60°x60°, 90° diagonal, up to 3.5 m). Pin and driver compatible with the VL53L5CX. Three cover 180° for SLAM, wall tracking and cliff detection on small robots.

Design notes

  • zones = 64 in 8x8 mode at up to 15 Hz; 4x4 (16-zone) mode runs up to 60 Hz. The ULD driver defaults to 4x4 at 1 Hz
  • 3.5 m is the 4x4 figure (dark, 88% white). In 8x8 it reaches only about 2.0 m dark and 0.5 m at 5 klux, so the wide FoV costs a lot of range in daylight
  • Accuracy ±11 mm below 0.2 m and ±5% beyond (dark, 8x8 at 15 Hz); ±9 mm / ±3% in 4x4 at 30 Hz
  • Pin-to-pin and driver compatible with the VL53L5CX: same pads, supplies and ULD API
  • No flash: the host uploads ~84 KB of firmware over I²C at every power-up (vl53l7cx_init in ST's ULD driver)
  • AVDD 2.8 V or 3.3 V (2.5-3.6 V) plus IOVDD 1.8, 2.8 or 3.3 V, so a single 3.3 V rail works. currentMa = AVDD 45 mA + IOVDD 50 mA typ while ranging
  • LPn (47k pull-up) disables I²C when low, for setting unique addresses (default 0x52). INT is open-drain (47k pull-up), I2C_RST goes to GND via 47k. I²C up to 1 MHz
  • FoV 60°x60° horizontal/vertical (90° diagonal); 940 nm VCSEL, Class 1 (IEC 60825-1:2014)
  • 16 pads at 0.75 mm pitch and a 2.316x1.2 mm thermal pad (B4) to GND. No KiCad footprint upstream
  • Pin table numbers 1-16 map to ST pads A1-A7, B1, B7, C1-C7 in that order
