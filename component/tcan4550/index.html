TCAN4550

Texas Instruments communication IC. Bosch M_CAN CAN FD controller and transceiver in one package, on SPI (18 MHz), with a 5 V LDO, WAKE pin and INH. Runs straight off a 12/24 V battery to give any MCU a CAN FD motor or sensor bus.

Design notes

  • VSUP must be 6-24 V and cannot run from a 5 V rail alone. The internal LDO makes the 5 V VCCOUT for the transceiver
  • VIO sets the 3.3 or 5 V logic level
  • Needs a 20 or 40 MHz crystal or clock on OSC1
  • Starts in standby and drops to sleep after about 4 min (tINACTIVE) unless the MCU services it
  • TI recommends the TCAN4550-Q1 as the drop-in upgrade
