Design notes

  • No built-in DC-DC: VCC2 (3-5.5 V) needs an isolated supply, e.g. a push-pull transformer driver
  • VCC1 runs at 1.71-1.89 V or 2.25-5.5 V
  • Connect both GND1 pins (2, 8) and both GND2 pins (9, 15)
  • ISO1430 (12 Mbit/s) and ISO1450 (50 Mbit/s) share the pinout
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
