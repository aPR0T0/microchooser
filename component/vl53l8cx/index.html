VL53L8CX

STMicroelectronics distance sensor. Newer 8x8-zone ToF sensor (45°x45° FoV, up to 4 m) with better range in ambient light than the VL53L5CX and an I²C or SPI interface. Needs 3.3 V and 1.8 V rails, and its I/O is 1.2/1.8 V only.

Design notes

  • zones = 64 in 8x8 mode at up to 15 Hz; 4x4 (16-zone) mode runs up to 60 Hz. The ULD driver defaults to 4x4 at 1 Hz
  • 4 m on 88% white in the dark in both 4x4 and 8x8. At 5 klux: 2.85 m in 4x4 and 1.55 m in 8x8 (the VL53L5CX manages 1.1 m)
  • Accuracy ±11 mm below 0.2 m and ±5% beyond (dark, 8x8 at 15 Hz); allow 1-2% extra for mounting tilt
  • Three supplies: AVDD 3.3 V (3.13-3.47 V), CORE_1V8 1.8 V and IOVDD 1.2 or 1.8 V. I/O is not 3.3 V tolerant (abs max 1.98 V), so level-shift from 3.3 V MCUs
  • currentMa = AVDD 43 mA + CORE_1V8 50 mA typ while ranging (215 mW continuous)
  • I²C up to 1 MHz (address 0x52) or SPI up to 3 MHz. The SPI_I2C_N pin selects SPI when high; in I²C mode, toggling it resets the I²C interface
  • No flash: the host uploads ~84 KB of firmware over I²C/SPI at every power-up (vl53l8cx_init in ST's ULD driver)
  • GPIO1 is the open-drain INT output (47k pull-up), GPIO2 is a SYNC input, and LPn disables I²C for address assignment
  • Same 6.4x3 mm outline and 0.75 mm pitch land pattern as the VL53L5CX, but the pin assignment differs, so it is not a drop-in replacement. Thermal pad (B4, 2.316x1.2 mm) goes to GND
  • Pin table numbers 1-16 map to ST pads A1-A7, B1, B7, C1-C7 in that order
  • FoV 45°x45° horizontal/vertical (65° diagonal); 940 nm VCSEL, Class 1 (IEC 60825-1:2014)
