Design notes

  • DTOF, 905 nm, IEC-60825 Class 1. Range 0.05–30 m on 90% white, 0.05–10 m on 10% black.
  • UART 1 Mbaud 8N1, 3.3 V TTL. Kit includes a USB adapter, but the sensor itself has no USB.
  • Single 4.9–5.2 V supply: 500 mA typ (600 max) at 10 Hz, 1.5 A at start-up.
  • No motor pin. Speed is set by serial command.
  • Datasheet: 0.12° angular resolution at 10 Hz; product page: 0.1125°. Accuracy ±30 mm typical, 13 mm resolution (90% target).
  • 80 klux ambient-light figure is from the product page. IP65, 0–50 °C (datasheet; the web page says -10 to 50 °C).
  • Weight 185 g per datasheet (190 g on spec page). ROS/ROS2 via rplidar_ros.
