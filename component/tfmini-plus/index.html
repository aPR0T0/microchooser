Design notes

  • Emitter is an 850 nm LED, not a laser (photobiological exemption group, EN 62471)
  • Range 0.1-4 m on 10% reflectivity; same 12 m / 4 m in 70 klux ambient light
  • Default 100 Hz; UART adjustable 1-1000 Hz (1000/n), I2C mode limited to 1-100 Hz. UART 115200 baud default
  • Ships in UART mode; switch to I2C by command (needs a USB-TTL adapter once)
  • 5 V ±0.5 V, average ≤110 mA, peak 140 mA, low-power mode <100 mW; 3.3 V LVTTL logic. IP65, -20 to 60 °C
