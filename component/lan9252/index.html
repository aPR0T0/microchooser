LAN9252

Microchip communication IC. 2/3-port EtherCAT slave controller (Beckhoff ESC) with two integrated 100BASE-TX PHYs; the host MCU talks to it over SPI/SQI or a parallel bus. The standard way to build EtherCAT servo drives, joint modules or I/O nodes for a robot arm.

Design notes

  • Host link: SPI/SQI (up to 80 MHz) or 8/16-bit HBI parallel bus; a 16-pin digital I/O mode needs no MCU at all
  • 3 FMMUs, 4 SyncManagers, 4 KB process RAM, distributed clocks; a third port is available as MII
  • Single 3.3 V supply with built-in 1.2 V regulator; VDDIO 1.62-3.6 V
  • Needs an I2C EEPROM for the ESC configuration and a 25 MHz crystal
  • channels = 2 EtherCAT ports
