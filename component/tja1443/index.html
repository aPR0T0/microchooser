NXP communication IC. CAN FD transceiver (5 Mbit/s) with sleep mode: battery-powered wake receiver, INH output to switch the node's regulator, WAKE pin and ERR_N diagnostics. For robots that power down whole nodes and wake them over CAN.

Design notes

  • Three supplies: VBAT 4.5-28 V (wake logic), VCC 4.5-5.5 V (transmitter), VIO 2.95-5.5 V (logic)
  • INH switches an external regulator off in sleep; wakes from the bus, the WAKE pin or the host
  • Drop-in replacement for the TJA1043
  • Bus pins rated -36/+40 V, not ±58 V
  • EN and STB_N select the mode; tie both high for normal mode if you don't need sleep
