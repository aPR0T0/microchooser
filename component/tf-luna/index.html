Design notes

  • Range 0.2-2.5 m on 10% reflectivity; datasheet A05 gives the same 8 m / 2.5 m figures outdoors at 90 klux (ambient immunity listed as 70 klux)
  • Default 100 Hz, max 250 Hz (500/n steps). UART 115200 baud default; I2C slave up to 400 kHz, default address 0x10
  • 3.3 V LVTTL logic; average current ≤70 mA, peak 150 mA; no enclosure (bare PCB + ABS/PC housing, not sealed)
  • Pin 5 picks the interface at power-up; pin 6 can be an on/off threshold output (I/O mode)
  • Datasheet lists 12.5 mm height; Benewake product page and Waveshare say 13.5 mm. Weight <5 g
