Design notes

  • End of life: DigiKey and Mouser list VL6180XV0NR/1 as obsolete (DigiKey suggests VL53L4CDV0DH/1), and Pololu's carrier is in end-of-life rationing. status is nrnd because the schema has no obsolete value
  • Ranging is specified for 0-100 mm. Beyond 100 mm it depends on the target and ambient light; breakout boards quote up to about 60 cm with range scaling
  • Worst case at 5 klux halogen (about 10-15 klux sunlight): >70 mm on white, >40 mm on a 3% target
  • rateHz = 10 is the datasheet's reference sampling rate (current and specs are quoted at 10 Hz). One range takes about 8-23 ms: 3.2 ms pre-calibration, up to 15 ms convergence and 4.3 ms averaging
  • The datasheet gives no ranging FoV. fovHDeg = 25 is the VCSEL emission angle (25° ±5°, 1/e²). The ALS angular response is 42° (half angle)
  • Accuracy: noise ≤2 mm (1σ) on a 17% target at 50 mm. Offset can shift up to 13 mm after reflow, so recalibrate in the final system
  • currentMa = 1.7 mA average at 10 Hz (17% target at 50 mm). During active ranging it draws AVDD 14 mA + AVDD_VCSEL 8 mA, with 38 mA peaks
  • Supply 2.6-3.0 V (2.7-2.9 V optimum), so it needs a 2.8 V rail rather than 3.3 V. I²C up to 400 kHz at 7-bit address 0x29. GPIO0 is chip enable (47k pull-up); GPIO1 is an open-drain interrupt
  • 850 nm VCSEL, Class 1 (IEC 60825-1:2007). The 16-bit ambient light sensor spans about 0.002 lux to 21 klux
  • 12 pads at 0.75 mm pitch, a different land pattern from the VL53Lxx parts. No upstream KiCad footprint
