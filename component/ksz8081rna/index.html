KSZ8081RNA

Microchip communication IC. Single-3.3 V 10/100 Ethernet PHY with RMII in a 4x4 mm QFN; makes the 50 MHz RMII clock from a 25 MHz crystal and has LinkMD cable diagnostics. A solid PHY for an MCU-based robot's Ethernet port.

Design notes

  • RNA uses a 25 MHz crystal and outputs the 50 MHz REF_CLK; the RND variant takes a 50 MHz clock instead
  • VDDIO is 3.3, 2.5 or 1.8 V (±5%); the 1.2 V core regulator is built in
  • Energy-detect power-down is off by default (register 0x18 bit 11)
  • Check silicon errata DS80000708
