Design notes

  • Max UART baud 2 Mbaud
  • 5 V mode: VCC 4.0-5.3 V, V3 to 0.1 µF only. 3.3 V mode: VCC 3.1-3.6 V with V3 tied to VCC
  • No VIO pin: UART levels follow VCC
  • Only -20..70 °C (CH340G/T are -40..85 °C)
  • Pin 7 is NC: leave it floating. No series resistors needed on UD+/UD-
  • Uses the CH341 VCP driver
