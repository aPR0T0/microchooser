Design notes

  • Nooploop sells through distributors (Buy&Contact on its site, no list price). Waveshare sells the same sensor under its own name
  • Datasheet V2.0 table gives 100 Hz for the F2 (series max 350 Hz on the older TOFSense-F). UART default 921600 baud; I2C address 0x08 + ID, up to 400 kHz
  • Datasheet gives no reflectivity for the 25 m figure (indoor tests). Std dev <1 cm to 10 m, <6 cm from 10-25 m; reads 0 m beyond 25 m, so check the status field
  • 905 nm laser: Nooploop says the Class 1 certification for 905/940 nm is under review; Waveshare lists it as Class 1. Older TOFSense-F / F P use a 750-830 nm Class 2 laser
  • 250 mW at 5 V (about 50 mA) in UART active output; 3.3 V TTL signals. TOFSense-F2 P (50 m) and F2 Mini (7.8 m) are separate models
