THVD1450

Design notes

  • Single 3-5.5 V supply: works directly with 3.3 V or 5 V MCUs
  • ±15 V extended common-mode range
  • Drop-in for the 75176/MAX485 SOIC-8 pinout
  • Open, short and idle bus fail-safe receiver
  • THVD1410 is the 500 kbit/s low-EMI sibling with the same pinout
