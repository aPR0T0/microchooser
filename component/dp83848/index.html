Design notes

  • MII or RMII, selected by strap. RMII mode needs a 50 MHz oscillator on X1 (no crystal); MII uses a 25 MHz crystal
  • Single 3.3 V supply, 3.3 V MAC interface only
  • Energy-detect mode idles at low power and wakes on cable activity
  • Auto-MDIX; rated for 150 m of cable
