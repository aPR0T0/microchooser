Design notes

  • Pin-compatible with the NXP TJA1042T/3 (TXD, GND, VCC, RXD, VIO, CANL, CANH, STB)
  • A-revision of the SIT1042 (better symmetry, lower emissions); the old SIT1042T/3 is hard to find
  • 5 V VCC; VIO for 3-5 V MCUs; STB high = standby with remote wake
  • SIT1051AT/3 is the silent-mode (no standby) sibling, like the TJA1051
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
