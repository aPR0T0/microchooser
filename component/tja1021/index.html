TJA1021

NXP communication IC. LIN 2.x / ISO 17987 transceiver (up to 20 kBd) with sleep mode, bus and local wake-up, and an INH output to switch the node's regulator. Drives cheap automotive LIN actuators and sensors from an MCU UART on a 12 V robot.

Design notes

  • No VIO pin: TXD/SLP_N inputs accept 3.3 V and 5 V logic; RXD is open-drain and needs a pull-up to the MCU rail
  • Commander (master) node needs an external 1 kΩ resistor and diode from VBAT to LIN
  • VBAT 5.5-27 V operating (timing specified to 18 V), 40 V abs max
  • Sleep current about 7 µA; wakes from the LIN bus or the WAKE_N pin
  • Pin-compatible with the TJA1020
