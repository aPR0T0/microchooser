Design notes

  • Triangulation scanner: 0.12–8 m on 80% reflectivity indoors. Error is ±2 cm up to 1 m and 3.5% relative from 1 to 6 m
  • Datasheet ambient light rating is 550 lux typical and 2000 lux max (for reference only), so treat it as indoor-only
  • UART is transmit-only (Tx, no Rx): 115200 baud 8N1 with 3.3 V logic (high level 2.4–3.5 V). Supply is 5 V (4.8–5.2 V)
  • Motor speed is set on M_CTR (0–3.3 V, default 1.8 V) with a voltage or a 10 kHz PWM. Higher voltage or duty means faster spin. Range is 5–8 Hz and 6 Hz is recommended (0.72° step)
  • Current is 300 mA typical and 500 mA max, with a ~1 A start-up peak. Use a 5 V 1 A supply, because a PC USB port alone may not be enough
  • Brushed motor: keep it away from silicone, sulfur or phosphorus outgassing materials (heat-sink glue, AB adhesive)
  • The included USB adapter board turns the UART into a USB serial port and drives the motor from DTR. The sensor itself has no USB
  • YDLIDAR now lists only the X2. The older X2L is sold by resellers with the same 3 kHz / 8 m specs and shares SDK settings with the X2
