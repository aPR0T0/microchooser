DFRobot HuskyLens (K210)

DFRobot camera. A no-code AI vision sensor with a K210 processor and 2" screen. It learns faces, objects, colours, lines and tags at the press of a button and sends bounding boxes to an Arduino, micro:bit or ESP32 over UART or I²C.

Design notes

  • Sends detection results, not images. Resolution and fps here are the OV2640 sensor's, and some units ship with a GC0328 sensor instead.
  • Runs on 3.3–5 V and draws 320 mA at 3.3 V (230 mA at 5 V) in face-recognition mode. Budget for it on battery robots.
  • HuskyLens 2 (SEN0638) is the faster successor.
