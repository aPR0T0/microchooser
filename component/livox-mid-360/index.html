Design notes

  • Ethernet/USB to a computer; no MCU device mapping. Data goes over 100BASE-TX Ethernet (Livox SDK 2 / ROS driver). Time sync is IEEE 1588 PTPv2 or GPS (PPS + 9600-baud NMEA on 3.3 V LVTTL pins)
  • Range is 70 m @80% and 40 m @10% reflectivity, both specified at 100 klx ambient. Blind zone is 0.1 m, and 0.1–0.2 m data is reference only
  • FOV is 360° horizontal and -7° to +52° vertical (59°). Repetitive or non-repetitive scanning is switchable, and coverage builds up over longer integration times
  • rateHz is the 10 Hz typical frame rate and zones = 200,000 pts/s (first return) ÷ 10 Hz. Distance random error (1σ) is ≤2 cm @10 m. Angular random error is ≤0.15°
  • Built-in IMU (ICM40609, 3-axis accelerometer + gyro), pushed at 200 Hz
  • Power is 6.5 W at 25 °C on 9–27 V DC, ≈540 mA at the recommended 12 V. Start-up draws 18 W for ~8 s below 35 °C, and self-heating can reach 14 W at -20 to 0 °C
  • IP67, -20 to 55 °C, 905 nm Class 1. The SensorLidar price of $749 is sensor-only ($799 with the splitter cable). The DJI Store doesn't sell in every region
  • Livox distributors now ship the Mid-360S as the current version (same form factor, FOV and driver compatibility)
