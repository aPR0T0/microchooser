MAX3485

Analog Devices (Maxim) communication IC. 3.3 V half-duplex RS-485/RS-422 transceiver, 10 Mbit/s, 32 nodes, standard 75176 pinout in SOIC-8. The classic way to put a 3.3 V MCU UART on a Modbus RTU or smart-servo bus.

Design notes

  • 3.3 V only (3.0-3.6 V)
  • One GPIO drives DE and /RE together for direction
  • A/B rated -7.5..+12.5 V abs max; no ESD rating in the datasheet (the MAX3485E is the ±15 kV version): add a TVS on field wiring
  • Shutdown (/RE high, DE low) but no bus wake-up
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
