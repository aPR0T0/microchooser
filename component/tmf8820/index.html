Design notes

  • Boots into a ROM bootloader: the host must download the ams measurement firmware (in the TMF882x driver package) into RAM over I²C after every power-up or EN low (~50 ms at 1 MHz I²C), then write the calibration data for the SPAD map in use.
  • FoV fields are the 3x3 wide mode, 41°x52° (63° diagonal). The default SPAD map is 3x3 at 33°x32° (45° diagonal).
  • Range: 5 m on 90% white (all zones) and 4-5 m on 18% grey under 350 lux LED light (3x3, 33°x32°, 30 Hz). Centre zone drops to 1 m on white at 10 klux sunlight-equivalent.
  • Accuracy ±3% at >=333 mm, ±10-19 mm closer. Reports the closest and second-closest object per zone, with a confidence (SNR) value.
  • VDD 2.7-3.5 V (3.0 V typ). I²C/INT pull-ups 1.62-3.5 V, up to 1 MHz, default address 0x41, I3C-tolerant.
  • 141 mW average (about 47 mA at 3 V) in default 3x3 mode at 30 Hz; 57 mA while the VCSEL is ranging; 19 mW in low-power 50 k-iteration mode; 2 µA with EN low. Operating range -40 to 85 °C.
