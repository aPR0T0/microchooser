Design notes

  • Range 0.1-7 m on 10% reflectivity; the same 12 m / 7 m figures hold in 70 klux ambient light
  • Default 100 Hz, adjustable 1-1000 Hz (1000/n). UART 115200 baud default; interface switched by command
  • Needs a tight 5 V ±0.1 V supply, no reverse-polarity or over-voltage protection; 3.3 V LVTTL logic
  • Average current ~50 mA in short-distance mode, ~140 mA in long-distance mode; peak 200 mA
  • Also has an I/O (on/off threshold) output mode on the TX line. Operating temperature 0-60 °C, not sealed
