Design notes

  • RT pin: float = 500 kHz, GND = 1.2 MHz, or a resistor for 200 kHz–2.2 MHz.
  • Pin 7 is soft-start (SS) on TPS62933; the TPS62933P variant has a power-good pin there instead, TPS62933F is forced PWM.
  • 0.5 mm pitch SOT-583: needs a fine-pitch stencil.
