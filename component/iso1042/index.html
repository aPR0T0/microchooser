Design notes

  • Bus side VCC2 (4.5-5.5 V) needs its own isolated 5 V supply (isolated DC-DC module or transformer driver)
  • Logic side VCC1 runs at 1.71-1.89 V or 2.25-5.5 V, so 1.8/3.3/5 V MCUs connect directly
  • No standby or silent pin
  • Not pin-compatible with the ISO1044
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
