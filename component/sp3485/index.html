Design notes

  • 3.3 V ±5% supply; logic inputs tolerate up to 6 V
  • Only ±2 kV HBM ESD: add a TVS (e.g. SM712) on A/B for off-board cables
  • Receiver fail-safe: output high for open or shorted inputs
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
