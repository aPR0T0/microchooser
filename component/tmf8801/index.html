Design notes

  • Range: 2.5 m on 18% grey or 88% white under ~350 lux; 1.25 m (18% grey) at 10 klux sunlight-equivalent, 0.55 m at 100 klux sunlight-equivalent.
  • Full 2.5 m needs the host to tune the internal oscillator to 4.7 MHz and load the latest ams firmware patch over I²C (reference driver). Calibration data, captured once in the final housing, must be written after every power-up.
  • Accuracy ±5% at >=200 mm, ±10 mm at 100-200 mm, ±15 mm at 20-100 mm.
  • FoV 24° FWHM in long-distance mode (37° at short distances); VCSEL field of illumination 21°.
  • Supply 2.7-3.3 V (3.0 V nominal, abs max 3.6 V). I²C up to Fast-mode Plus, default address 0x41 (changeable after power-up; use EN to bring sensors up one by one). Input thresholds are specified for a 1.8 V pull-up; SDA/SCL/INT/EN tolerate up to 3.6 V.
  • 27 mA average at 30 Hz default settings, 0.23 mA at 1 Hz, 0.26 µA with EN low. Operating range -30 to 70 °C.
  • No official KiCad symbol or footprint. Datasheet land pattern: 2 rows of 6 pads, 0.35 x 0.575 mm, 0.6 mm pitch. A pin table is provided.
