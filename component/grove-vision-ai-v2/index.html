Seeed Grove Vision AI Module V2

Seeed Studio camera. A low-cost TinyML vision board with a Himax WiseEye2 HX6538 (dual Cortex-M55 + Ethos-U55 NPU) and a CSI port for an OV5647 camera. Runs YOLO/MobileNet models and reports results to an MCU over I²C (Grove) or UART.

Design notes

  • Needs a Raspberry Pi-style OV5647 CSI camera. The resolution, fps and 62° FOV here are for the OV5647-62 camera, and inference runs on downscaled frames.
  • Models are deployed with SenseCraft AI (TensorFlow/PyTorch, YOLOv5/v8, MobileNet). Has up to 2432 KB of system memory, a PDM microphone and an SD slot.
  • Runs on 5 V. The board size is measured from Seeed's dimension drawing (about 40×20 mm including USB-C).
