Design notes

  • 60 fps is the processing rate. getFPS() reports 2–62 fps depending on light. Results come back as blocks and vectors, not full-resolution images.
  • Lens is 60°×40° (H×V). Draws 140 mA typical from USB 5 V or 6–10 V unregulated input.
  • Uses an NXP LPC4330 (204 MHz dual-core) with 264 KB RAM and 2 MB flash. Also has digital and analog outputs.
