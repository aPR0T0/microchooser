ENC28J60

Microchip communication IC. Stand-alone 10BASE-T Ethernet MAC+PHY on SPI with an 8 KB packet buffer. Adds basic wired Ethernet to MCUs without a MAC; fine for slow telemetry or config links, but needs a software TCP/IP stack (lwIP).

Design notes

  • 10 Mbit/s only, but works on any 10/100/1000 network
  • SPI up to 20 MHz; 3.1-3.6 V supply, only RESET/CS/SCK/SI are 5 V tolerant
  • Draws about 160 mA while transmitting; needs a 25 MHz crystal, 10 µF VCAP cap and magnetics
  • Power-save sleep exits only via SPI (no wake on link)
  • No on-chip TCP/IP stack, unlike the W5500. Check the silicon errata
