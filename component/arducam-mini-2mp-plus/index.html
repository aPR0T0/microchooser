Design notes

  • SPI is limited to 8 MHz, so real frame rates over SPI are far below the sensor's 15 fps at UXGA. Best for stills and slow streaming.
  • 5 V and 3.3 V tolerant I/O. Draws 70 mA at 5 V, or 20 mA in low-power mode.
