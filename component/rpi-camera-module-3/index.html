Design notes

  • 4.74 mm F1.8 lens, 66°×41° (H×V), 75° diagonal. Focus range 10 cm to infinity.
  • Sensor modes: 4608×2592 at 14 fps, 2304×1296 at 56 fps (30 fps HDR), 1536×864 at 120 fps.
  • 200 mm 15-pin cable included. Pi 5, Zero and CM IO boards need a 22-to-15-pin cable. In production until at least January 2030.
