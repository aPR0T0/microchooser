MCP2515

Microchip communication IC. Classic CAN 2.0B controller on SPI (1 Mbit/s, 3 TX / 2 RX buffers, 6 filters). Needs an external CAN transceiver. Adds a CAN port to MCUs or SBCs without one, for legacy CAN motor drivers and sensors.

Design notes

  • Controller only: pair it with a CAN transceiver (TXCAN/RXCAN go to the transceiver's TXD/RXD)
  • SPI up to 10 MHz, modes 0,0 and 1,1
  • Oscillator max 40 MHz only at VDD 4.5-5.5 V; 25 MHz max over 2.7-5.5 V
  • Sleep mode wakes on CAN bus activity (WAKIF) and comes back in Listen-Only mode: the MCU must set the mode again
  • Classic CAN only: no CAN FD frames
