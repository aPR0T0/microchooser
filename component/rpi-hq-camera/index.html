Design notes

  • Needs a lens, so the field of view depends on the lens you fit. Back focus is 12.5–22.4 mm (CS) or 2.6–11.8 mm (M12).
  • Sensor modes: 4056×3040 at 10 fps, 2028×1520 at 40 fps, 2028×1080 at 50 fps, 1332×990 at 120 fps.
  • The IR-cut filter can be removed for IR sensitivity, but the change is irreversible. Has a 1/4"-20 tripod mount and 200 mm cable.
