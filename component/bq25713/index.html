Design notes

  • Controller: four external NMOS FETs, inductor and sense resistors; charge starts at 0 A until the host writes ChargeCurrent().
  • No TS/NTC pin: monitor battery temperature with the MCU.
  • Pin-compatible with BQ25703A; BQ25713B is the variant with different defaults.
