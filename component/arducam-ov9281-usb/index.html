Design notes

  • Needs a USB host such as a Raspberry Pi, Jetson or PC (UVC, no driver needed). It can't connect directly to an MCU camera port.
  • 100 fps is MJPEG only. Uncompressed YUY2 runs at 10 fps.
  • 5 V USB power, 200 mA max.
