AX58100

ASIX communication IC. 2/3-port EtherCAT slave controller with two 100BASE-TX PHYs plus on-chip PWM, step/dir, encoder input and SPI master. Can run a simple EtherCAT motor or joint node with little MCU code.

Design notes

  • 8 FMMUs, 8 SyncManagers, 9 KB RAM, 64-bit distributed clocks; third port as MII
  • Host link: SPI slave (up to 30 MHz) or 8/16-bit async local bus
  • Motion extras: 3-ch PWM, step/dir, ABZ/Hall encoder, emergency-stop input, SPI master for ADC/DAC
  • Needs 3.3 V plus an external 1.2 V core rail (no internal regulator)
  • Needs an I2C EEPROM and a 25 MHz crystal; 0.4 mm lead pitch
  • epMm is the midpoint of the drawing's min/max
