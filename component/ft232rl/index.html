FT232RL

FTDI communication IC. Classic USB full-speed to UART bridge (up to 3 Mbaud) with internal EEPROM, clock and CBUS I/O, VCCIO 1.8-5.25 V. End of life: use the pin-compatible FT232RNL for new robot host-link designs.

Design notes

  • EOL: FTDI last-time-buy was 1 Jun 2024; the FT232RNL is the pin-to-pin replacement
  • Max 3 Mbaud
  • VCC 4.0-5.25 V with the internal oscillator (3.3 V operation needs an external 12 MHz crystal)
  • VCCIO sets the UART level: tie it to 3V3OUT for 3.3 V I/O
  • A CBUS pin can be set as TXDEN for RS-485 auto direction
