Design notes

  • Laser triangulation (not DTOF), 785 nm; indoor use or outdoors without direct sunlight.
  • UART 115200 baud 8N1, 3.3 V TTL (RX recognised by current, node voltage ≥1.6 V). USB comes from the separate CP2102 adapter board in the dev kit.
  • Separate supplies: scanner core 4.9–5.5 V (300 mA working), motor 5–9 V (~100 mA at 5 V). Datasheet: supply ~9 V to the motor to scan faster than 5.5 Hz.
  • CTRL_MOTO pin takes a PWM / enable signal for motor speed; scan rate 1–10 Hz, 5.5 Hz typical.
  • Datasheet quotes angular resolution ≤1° at 5.5 Hz; at 8 kHz sampling the point spacing works out to ~0.25°.
  • Slamtec spec page lists 100 mA system current; the datasheet's per-rail figures (300 mA core + 100 mA motor) are used here.
  • No black-target range is given. Accuracy and range-resolution figures are from Slamtec's A1 spec page.
  • ROS/ROS2: Slamtec rplidar_ros; SDK: github.com/Slamtec/rplidar_sdk.
