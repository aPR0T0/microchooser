Design notes

  • LED emitter, not a laser. Garmin's manual gives no laser class; SparkFun describes the 940 nm LED as Class 1
  • Signal pins are 3.3 V max (5 V on SDA/SCL damages it) even though VIN needs 4.75-5.25 V. Internal 13 kΩ pull-ups, I2C address 0x62, 400 kHz
  • Update rate: >200 Hz typical over I2C, up to 200 Hz over ANT; Garmin states no default rate. Accuracy figures are 1σ repeatability in high-accuracy mode on a 90% target
  • Also does ANT wireless and can be set up for SPI with custom nRF52840 firmware (Nordic SDK); only I2C is listed here
  • 2 mA idle, 85 mA during an acquisition. SparkFun sells a Qwiic version (SEN-18009) that runs from 3.3 V
