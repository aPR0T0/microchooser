Design notes

  • Single 3.0-3.6 V supply; logic levels follow VCC
  • Rs pin 8: GND = full speed, resistor to GND = slope control, high = standby
  • Standby is listen-only (driver off, receiver on, 370 µA): the MCU watches RXD and pulls Rs low to transmit
  • Bus pins rated only -4..+16 V abs max: a short to 24 V will kill it. Common-mode range -2..+7 V
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
