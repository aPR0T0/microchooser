MAX3232E

Texas Instruments communication IC. 3-5.5 V dual RS-232 driver/receiver with a built-in charge pump (4 small caps), 250 kbit/s, ±15 kV ESD on line pins. Connects an MCU UART to RS-232 gear: GPS, lidars, legacy motor controllers, PCs.

Design notes

  • 2 drivers + 2 receivers (TX/RX plus RTS/CTS)
  • VCC 3.0-3.6 V or 4.5-5.5 V. Caps: 4 x 0.1 µF at 3.3 V
  • 250 kbit/s typ, 150 kbit/s guaranteed into 3 kΩ / 1000 pF
  • Line outputs rated ±13.2 V, receiver inputs ±25 V
  • Same pinout as the generic MAX3232
