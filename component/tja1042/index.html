Design notes

  • 5 V VCC; VIO (2.8-5.5 V) on the /3 variants only; on TJA1042T (no /3) pin 5 is SPLIT
  • STB high = standby (about 10 µA) with bus wake-up reported on RXD; tie low for normal mode
  • AEC-Q100, ±58 V on CANH/CANL
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
