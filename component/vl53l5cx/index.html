Design notes

  • zones = 64 in 8x8 mode at up to 15 Hz; 4x4 (16-zone) mode runs up to 60 Hz. The ULD driver defaults to 4x4 at 1 Hz
  • Max range 4 m in 4x4 (dark, 88% white). In 8x8: 3.5 m dark, about 1.1 m at 5 klux, 1.3 m on 17% grey
  • Accuracy ±15 mm below 0.2 m and ±5% from 0.2 to 4 m (dark, 8x8 at 15 Hz); up to ±11% on grey at 5 klux
  • No flash: the host uploads ~84 KB of firmware over I²C at every power-up (vl53l5cx_init in ST's ULD driver). Budget MCU flash and boot time
  • AVDD 2.8 V or 3.3 V (2.5-3.6 V) plus IOVDD 1.8, 2.8 or 3.3 V, so a single 3.3 V rail works. currentMa = AVDD 45 mA + IOVDD 50 mA typ while ranging (313 mW at 3.3/3.3 V)
  • LPn (47k pull-up) disables I²C when low; use it to give each sensor its own address (default 0x52). INT is open-drain (47k pull-up), I2C_RST goes to GND via 47k. I²C up to 1 MHz
  • FoV 45°x45° horizontal/vertical (63° diagonal); 940 nm VCSEL, Class 1 (IEC 60825-1:2014)
  • 16 pads at 0.75 mm pitch (two rows of 7 plus 2 end pads) and a 2.316x1.2 mm thermal pad (B4) that must go to GND. No KiCad footprint upstream, so use ST's CAD files
  • Pin table numbers 1-16 map to ST pads A1-A7, B1, B7, C1-C7 in that order
  • Optical part: use a no-wash process; MSL3
