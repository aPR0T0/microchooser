Design notes

  • No enable or power-good pin.
  • The metal tab is SW, not GND: don't pour ground under it.
  • 8 V minimum input; needs a Schottky diode (the datasheet uses a 10 A / 45 V MBRD1045G) and a 47 µH-class inductor.
