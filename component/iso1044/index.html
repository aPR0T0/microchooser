Design notes

  • Bus side VCC2 (4.5-5.5 V) needs an isolated 5 V supply; VCC1 takes 1.71-1.89 V or 2.25-5.5 V logic
  • Narrow body: 3 kVrms basic isolation, less creepage than the wide-body ISO1042
  • Common-mode range only ±12 V (ISO1042: ±30 V)
  • No standby or silent pin
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
