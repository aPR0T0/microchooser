Design notes

  • No battery protection: pair with DW01A + FS8205A (like the common 03962A module).
  • Linear: at 1 A from 5 V it burns ~1 W and folds back without a large ground pad.
  • Ground TEMP to disable the NTC check.
  • Abs max VCC is 8 V.
