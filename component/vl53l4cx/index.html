Design notes

  • 6 m max on 88% white indoors at 50% detection rate (5 m at 90%), 2.5 m on 17% grey. Outdoor overcast (~5 klux): 1.8 m white, 1.3 m grey. All at a 33 ms timing budget
  • Histogram processing reports up to 4 targets per measurement, ignores cover-glass crosstalk beyond 80 cm and compensates smudge below 80 cm
  • Detects from 0 mm and is linear from 10 mm
  • rateHz = 30 is the driver default (33 ms per ranging). The datasheet quotes no higher max
  • FoV 18° is the detection-volume cone at 1 m (22° at 100 mm). ST does not call it diagonal
  • Accuracy (white, indoor): ±8 mm at 10-110 mm, ±3% above 110 mm. Outdoor overcast: ±5% (white) to ±8% (grey)
  • currentMa = 19 mA typ (21 max) active-ranging average including the VCSEL. Peaks reach 40 mA
  • AVDD 2.6-3.5 V (2.8 V typ), IOVDD 1.6-3.5 V. I²C up to 1 MHz at address 0x52, plus XSHUT and an open-drain GPIO1 interrupt. Drivers are compatible with the VL53L3CX
  • Pin-to-pin compatible with the VL53L0X/VL53L1X/VL53L4CD. ST's PCB pattern (0.5 mm pads, 0.8 mm pitch, 3.7x2.1 mm) matches KiCad ST_VL53L1x. 940 nm VCSEL, Class 1 (IEC 60825-1:2014)
