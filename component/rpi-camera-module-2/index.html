Design notes

  • Fixed-focus 3.04 mm F2.0 lens, 62.2°×48.8° (H×V).
  • Modes: 3280×2464 at 21 fps, 1080p47, 1640×1232p41, 640×480p206. Recent kernels give about 103 fps at VGA in 10-bit mode.
  • Pi 5, Zero and CM IO boards need a 22-to-15-pin cable. In production until at least January 2028.
