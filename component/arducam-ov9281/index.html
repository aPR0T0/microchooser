Design notes

  • RAW8 modes: 1280×800 at 143 fps, 1280×720 at 171 fps, 640×400 at 309 fps. RAW10 is slower (114 fps at full resolution).
  • No IR-cut filter, so it pairs well with 850 nm IR illumination. Mono output only.
  • The 22-pin 0.5 mm FPC on the module is the Pi 5/CM pitch. Older Pis need a 22-to-15-pin cable.
