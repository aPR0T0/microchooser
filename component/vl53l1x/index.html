STMicroelectronics distance sensor. Long-distance single-zone ToF: up to 4 m in the dark at up to 50 Hz. A programmable ROI can narrow the 27° FoV or steer it. Popular for robot obstacle detection, drone altitude hold and people sensing over I²C.

Design notes

  • 4 m needs long distance mode, a 140 ms timing budget, a white target and no IR. At 100 ms on white: 3.6 m in the dark, 1.66 m at 50 kcps/SPAD (sunny day behind a window), 0.73 m at 200 kcps/SPAD (direct sun through a window)
  • Short distance mode tops out at 1.3 m but is nearly immune to ambient light (still 1.3 m at 200 kcps/SPAD)
  • rateHz = 50 is ST's max ranging frequency (20 ms budget, short mode only). 33 ms is the shortest budget that works in every mode
  • FoV 27° is diagonal (ST: receiver diagonal FoV, programmable 15-27° via ROI). The ROI can be moved across the 16x16 SPAD array for crude multizone scanning
  • Accuracy is ST's ranging error (accuracy + repeatability), long mode at 100 ms: ±20 mm in the dark, ±25 mm in ambient light
  • Minimum ranging distance 4 cm. Closer targets are detected but not measured accurately
  • currentMa = 16 mA typ (18 max) ranging average in long mode, including the VCSEL. Peaks reach 40 mA
  • AVDD 2.6-3.5 V. I/O defaults to 1.8 V mode or can run in 2V8 mode. I²C up to 400 kHz at programmable address 0x52, plus XSHUT and a GPIO1 interrupt
  • Pin-to-pin compatible with the VL53L0X. 940 nm VCSEL, Class 1 (IEC 60825-1:2014)
