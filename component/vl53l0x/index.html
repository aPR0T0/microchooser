VL53L0X

STMicroelectronics distance sensor. Classic single-zone ToF ranger: up to 2 m (1.2 m in default mode) with a 25° cone over I²C. Cheap and well supported by Arduino/Pololu/Adafruit libraries for robot obstacle detection; the VL53L1X/L4CX reach further.

Design notes

  • 2 m on 88% white needs the long-range profile and no IR ambient. The default profile gives 1.2 m (white), 80 cm on 17% grey. Outdoor overcast (~5 klux): 80 cm white, 50 cm grey
  • rateHz = 50 is the max from ST's example profiles (high-speed, 20 ms budget, ±5%). The default 33 ms budget gives about 30 Hz
  • FoV 25° is ST's system FoV (cone), not a diagonal figure
  • Accuracy is 1σ with a 33 ms budget: 4% at 1.2 m white, 7% at 70 cm grey indoors (7%/12% outdoors). The high-accuracy profile (200 ms) gives under ±3%
  • The datasheet gives no minimum distance. rangeMinM = 0.05 because its ranging plots start at 50 mm
  • currentMa = 19 mA active-ranging average including the VCSEL (33 ms budget). Peaks reach 40 mA
  • AVDD 2.6-3.5 V (2.8 V typ). I/O defaults to 1.8 V mode; the API can switch it to 2V8 mode. I²C up to 400 kHz at address 0x52 (8-bit, 0x29 7-bit), which software can change. Use XSHUT to give several sensors different addresses
  • Still Active at ST, with no NRND notice found. The pin-compatible VL53L4CD/VL53L4CX are the newer parts
  • Same 12-pad, 0.8 mm pitch land pattern as the VL53L1X (KiCad ST_VL53L1x). 940 nm VCSEL, Class 1 (IEC 60825-1:2014)
