Design notes

  • Triangulation scanner: 0.12–10 m on 80% reflectivity indoors. Error is ±2 cm up to 1 m and 3.5% relative from 1 to 6 m
  • Scan rate is 6–12 Hz. The datasheet's 'typical' column uses 7 Hz (0.50° step), so rateHz/zones assume 7 Hz. Steps are 0.43° at 6 Hz and 0.86° at 12 Hz
  • UART is transmit-only: 128000 baud 8N1 (an unusual rate; check that your MCU/USB bridge supports it), 3.3 V logic. Supply is 5 V (4.8–5.2 V)
  • M_CTR is inverted compared with the X2: lower voltage or duty means faster spin, and 0 V or 0% duty gives max speed. It takes a 10 kHz PWM (65% duty typical) or 0–3.3 V (default 2.15 V)
  • Current is 330 mA typical and 380 mA max, with an 800 mA–1 A start-up peak. Use a 5 V 1 A supply
  • Ambient light is rated to 40 klux max (2 klux typical, reference only). Brushed motor with a 1500 h rated service life
  • No USB on the sensor. YDLIDAR kits usually add a USB adapter board, but some listings ship only the PH2.0-8P cable
