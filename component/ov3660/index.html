Design notes

  • Pin-compatible with OV2640 24-pin modules, but it has a different register map and needs driver support.
  • Max frame rates: 2048×1536 15 fps, 1080p 20 fps, 720p 45 fps, VGA 60 fps, QVGA 120 fps.
  • Shipped on newer XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense boards in place of the OV2640. No autofocus.
