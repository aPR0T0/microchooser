MCP2562FD

Microchip communication IC. CAN FD transceiver with a 1.8-5.5 V VIO pin and 5 µA standby with bus wake. Works straight from 1.8/3.3 V MCUs; a common pick for CAN FD links to motor drivers and sensor nodes.

Design notes

  • 5 V VDD; VIO 1.8-5.5 V
  • Marketed for 2/5/8 Mbit/s but only 5 Mbit/s is guaranteed in the datasheet
  • STBY high = standby with wake-up on RXD; tie low for normal mode
  • MCP2561FD is the same die with SPLIT instead of VIO on pin 5
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
