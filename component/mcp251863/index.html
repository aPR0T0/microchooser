MCP251863

Microchip communication IC. MCP2518FD CAN FD controller plus ATA6563 transceiver in one package, SPI to the host, 5 Mbit/s data phase. A one-chip CAN FD port to motor controllers from a 3.3 V MCU (the transceiver needs 5 V).

Design notes

  • Three supplies: transceiver VCC 4.5-5.5 V, VIO 2.8-5.5 V, controller VDD 2.7-5.5 V
  • Controller TXCAN/RXCAN and transceiver TXD/RXD are separate pins: wire them together on the PCB
  • STBY has an internal pull-up, so the part stays in standby until STBY is pulled low
  • Bus pins rated -27..+42 V DC (asymmetric)
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
