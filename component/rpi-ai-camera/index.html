Design notes

  • Modes: 4056×3040 at 10 fps, 2028×1520 binned at 30 fps. The model input is up to 640×640 int8, with about 8 MB for firmware, weights and working memory.
  • Manual focus from 20 cm to infinity. 4.74 mm F1.79 lens, 66.3°×52.3° (H×V), 78.3° diagonal. Has an IR-cut filter.
  • An onboard RP2040 manages the network firmware. Works with every Raspberry Pi that has a CSI connector.
