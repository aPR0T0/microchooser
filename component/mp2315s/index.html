Design notes

  • Next generation of the MP2315 (which is NRND); same TSOT-23-8 pinout.
  • AAM pin: resistor divider = power-save at light load, tie to VCC or float = forced CCM.
  • BST needs a capacitor plus a series resistor (10 Ω in every datasheet circuit).
