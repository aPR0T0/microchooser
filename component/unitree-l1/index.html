Unitree 4D LiDAR L1

Unitree Robotics distance sensor. Low-cost 360°×90° hemispherical 3D LiDAR (21,600 pts/s, up to 30 m) with a built-in IMU, for SLAM, mapping and obstacle avoidance on legged and wheeled robots. Point-LIO SLAM is supported.

Design notes

  • Ethernet/USB to a computer; no MCU device mapping. The L1 itself only has a 3.3 V TTL UART at 2,000,000 baud. The included adapter module (CP210x, USB Type-C) and 12 V adapter connect it to a PC or SBC
  • Range is up to 30 m @90% reflectivity on the RM (15 m @10%). The PM reaches 20 m (10 m @10%). Near blind zone is 0.05 m (reference only)
  • rateHz is the 11 Hz horizontal (azimuth) rotation and zones = 21,600 effective pts/s ÷ 11 Hz. Raw sampling is 43,200 pts/s and vertical mirror scan is 180 Hz. Point density is highest near the centre of the FOV
  • Built-in IMU (3-axis accelerometer + 3-axis gyro), sampled at 1 kHz and reported at 250 Hz
  • Power is 6 W at 25 °C on 12 V DC (≈500 mA). Unitree specifies a 12 V 1 A supply
  • Output is 3D position + 1D grayscale. Rated to 100 klux strong light, IP54, -10 to 60 °C. 905 nm with 25 W peak pulsed laser power, Class 1
  • The official store currently shows the L1 as unavailable. The L2 (Ethernet, 64,000 pts/s) is the successor
