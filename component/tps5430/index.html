Design notes

  • Needs an external Schottky diode (e.g. B340A).
  • Fixed 500 kHz voltage-mode PWM with no light-load mode: ~2 mA quiescent.
  • Pins 2 and 3 are NC; the PowerPAD must be soldered to GND.
