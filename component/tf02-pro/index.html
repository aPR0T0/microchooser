Design notes

  • Range 0.1-13.5 m on 10% reflectivity; same 40 m / 13.5 m outdoors at 100 klux
  • Default 100 Hz, adjustable 1-1000 Hz (2000/n). UART 115200 baud; I2C address 0x01-0x7F; I/O threshold output on the green wire
  • DC 5-12 V supply but 3.3 V LVTTL signals; average ≤200 mA, peak 300 mA. No reverse-polarity protection
  • Repeatability 1σ <2 cm (0.1-35 m on 90% target). IP65, -20 to 60 °C; 50 g including cable
  • Older listings (RobotShop) call it an LED rangefinder; current Benewake datasheet A02 says VCSEL. RS-485/CAN versions are the TF02-Pro-i / TF02-i
