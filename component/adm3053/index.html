Analog Devices communication IC. Isolated classic CAN transceiver with a built-in isoPower DC-DC (2.5 kVrms, 1 Mbit/s): one 5 V rail powers both sides, no separate isolated supply. Good for CANopen nodes on robot arms. Not CAN FD.

Design notes

  • Single 5 V VCC feeds the internal isolated DC-DC; VIO (3.0-5.5 V) sets the MCU logic level
  • isoPower switches at high frequency: follow ADI AN-0971 layout guidance for radiated emissions
  • RS pin must not float: GND = full speed, resistor = slope control, high = standby
  • Classic CAN only, -40..85 °C
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
