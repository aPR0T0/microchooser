ISOW1044

Texas Instruments communication IC. 5 kVrms isolated CAN FD transceiver (5 Mbit/s, ±58 V) with a built-in isolated DC-DC, a spare isolated GPIO and standby with bus wake-up. A one-chip isolated CAN FD node for motor drives on a 5 V rail.

Design notes

  • VDD 4.5-5.5 V powers the DC-DC; VIO runs at 1.71-1.89 V or 2.25-5.5 V
  • Some pins must be joined on the PCB: GNDIO to GND1, VISOOUT to VSIN/VISOIN, GND2 to GISOIN
  • STB high or floating = standby with WUP remote wake; tie STB low for normal mode
  • About 20 mA of spare isolated output for bus-side circuits
  • Often out of stock at distributors; TI suggests the ISOW1050 as an alternative
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
