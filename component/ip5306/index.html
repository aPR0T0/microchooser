Design notes

  • The boost shuts off after 32 s below ~45 mA load: an MCU in sleep can lose power (the IP5306-I2C variant can disable this).
  • LED1–3 are charlieplexed fuel-gauge LED drivers, not logic outputs.
  • Rated 0–70 °C ambient.
