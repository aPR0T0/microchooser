Design notes

  • zones = 18: the 3x6 mode (33°x60° FoV) gives 18 zones, which ams lists as the maximum. The 4x4 mode gives 16 zones at 41°x52° (63° diagonal); the FoV fields are that mode.
  • 4x4 and 3x6 are time-multiplexed (two VCSEL bursts per result), so they run at about 15 Hz with the default 550 k iterations. The default 3x3 mode (33°x32°) runs at 30 Hz.
  • Boots into a ROM bootloader: the host must download the ams measurement firmware (in the TMF882x driver package) into RAM over I²C after every power-up or EN low (~50 ms at 1 MHz I²C), then write the calibration data for the SPAD map in use.
  • Range in 4x4 mode, 90% white, 350 lux LED: 5 m centre zones but only 2.9-3 m edge and corner zones; 18% grey 4 m centre, 1.4-1.5 m edge. Centre zone 1 m at 10 klux sunlight-equivalent.
  • Accuracy ±3% at >=333 mm, ±10-19 mm closer. Reports the closest and second-closest object per zone, with a confidence (SNR) value.
  • VDD 2.7-3.5 V (3.0 V typ). I²C/INT pull-ups 1.62-3.5 V, up to 1 MHz, default address 0x41, I3C-tolerant.
  • 141 mW average (about 47 mA at 3 V) in default 3x3 mode at 30 Hz; 57 mA while the VCSEL is ranging; 2 µA with EN low. Operating range -40 to 85 °C.
