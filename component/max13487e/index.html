MAX13487E

Analog Devices (Maxim) communication IC. 5 V half-duplex RS-485 transceiver with AutoDirection: no DE pin, the chip switches direction itself. 500 kbit/s, 128 nodes, ±15 kV ESD. Handy for UART-only links to Modbus devices or smart servos.

Design notes

  • Auto-direction: no DE pin or GPIO needed; DI must idle high (a UART does this)
  • Needs a pull-up on A and pull-down on B so A-B > 200 mV when the bus idles
  • 5 V only: RO swings to VCC, so level-shift it into a 3.3 V MCU
  • /RE low = always receive (you hear your own TX); /SHDN low = 10 µA shutdown
  • MAX13488E is the 16 Mbit/s version
