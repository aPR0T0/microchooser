CH9102F

WCH communication IC. Crystal-free USB full-speed to UART bridge, up to 4 Mbaud, RTS/CTS flow control and a separate 1.8-5 V VIO. Good host USB link to 1.8/3.3/5 V robot MCUs; the TNOW pin can drive an RS-485 transceiver's DE.

Design notes

  • Max UART baud 4 Mbaud (avoid sustained full-duplex traffic at 3 Mbaud and above)
  • VDD5 4.0-5.5 V uses the internal LDO; for 3.0-3.6 V tie V3 to VDD5
  • VIO 1.7-5.5 V powers the UART pins: tie it to the MCU rail
  • WAKEUP pin for USB remote wake-up; SUSPEND outputs
  • Works with the OS built-in CDC driver
