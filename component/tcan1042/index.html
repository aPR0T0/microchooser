Design notes

  • 5 V VCC; VIO (3.0-5.5 V) sets the TXD/STB/RXD levels, so it talks directly to 3.3 V MCUs
  • Only the G parts (TCAN1042HGVDR) are rated 5 Mbit/s; TCAN1042HVDR is rated 2 Mbit/s
  • STB high = low-power standby with remote wake-up; tie it low for normal mode
  • Same pinout as the TJA1042T/3
  • Needs 120 Ω termination at both bus ends
