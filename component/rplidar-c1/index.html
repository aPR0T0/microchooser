Design notes

  • DTOF, 905 nm, IEC-60825 Class 1. Outputs reflectivity alongside distance.
  • Black (10%) target: 0.05–6 m. White (70%) target: 0.05–12 m.
  • UART 460800 baud 8N1, 3.3 V TTL. USB only through the bundled CP2102 adapter.
  • One 4.8–5.2 V rail for core and motor: 230 mA typ (260 max) at 10 Hz, 800 mA at start-up; ripple ≤150 mV.
  • No motor-control pin. Speed (8–12 Hz) is set by serial command, and the motor runs only while scanning.
  • Compatible with the S-series protocol and the A-series standard protocol; needs a current rplidar_sdk / rplidar_ros (ROS and ROS2).
  • IP54, -10 to 40 °C.
