Design notes

  • 1.3 m max on 88% white indoors at 50% detection rate (1.2 m at 90%), 475 mm on 17% grey. Outdoor overcast (~5 klux): 600 mm white, 450 mm grey. All at a 33 ms timing budget
  • Detects from 0 mm and is linear from 1 mm, so there is no blind zone for cliff or edge sensing
  • rateHz = 100 is ST's max ranging frequency. Datasheet performance is specified at a 33 ms timing budget
  • FoV 18° is the detection-volume cone at 1 m (22° at 100 mm). ST does not call it diagonal
  • Accuracy (white, indoor): ±7 mm at 1-100 mm, ±8 mm at 101-200 mm, ±3% above 200 mm. Outdoor overcast: ±8%
  • currentMa = 22 mA typ (24 max) active-ranging average including the VCSEL. Peaks reach 40 mA. Autonomous low-power mode wakes the host with a distance-threshold interrupt
  • AVDD 2.6-3.5 V (2.8 V typ), IOVDD 1.6-3.5 V. I²C up to 1 MHz at address 0x52. XSHUT and the open-drain GPIO1 interrupt take 10k pull-ups
  • Pin-to-pin compatible with the VL53L0X/VL53L1X/VL53L4CX. ST's PCB pattern (0.5 mm pads, 0.8 mm pitch, 3.7x2.1 mm, 5+1+5+1 layout) matches KiCad ST_VL53L1x
  • 940 nm VCSEL, Class 1 (IEC 60825-1:2014). Suggested by distributors as the replacement for the obsolete VL6180X
