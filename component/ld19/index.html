LDROBOT LD19

LDROBOT distance sensor. Walnut-sized 360° DTOF LiDAR (D300 kit): 12 m, 4.5 kHz at 10 Hz, 30 klux, 5 V/180 mA. Streams scans over a TX-only UART with no commands needed, so it is easy to read from an MCU. Being replaced by the STL-19P.

Design notes

  • NRND: LDROBOT's site no longer lists it. Waveshare and The Pi Hut mark the D300/LD19 discontinued and point to the D500 kit (STL-19P); DFRobot SKU SEN0547 is now sold as STL-19P. youyeetoo still had stock.
  • DTOF, 905 nm, IEC-60825 Class 1. Range 0.02–12 m on a 70% target; no black-target figure given.
  • One-way UART 230400 baud 8N1, 3.3 V. Starts streaming 0x54-header packets once rotation is stable, and has no RX line.
  • PWM pin: ground it for the internal 10 Hz default. External 20–50 kHz PWM (45–55% duty for ≥100 ms to engage) gives 5–13 Hz.
  • 4.5–5.5 V, 180 mA working, 300 mA at start-up.
  • Accuracy ±45 mm mean over 0.3–12 m, 10 mm std dev; below 0.3 m data is output but not guaranteed.
  • Size: Waveshare/youyeetoo give 54 × 46.29 × 34.8 mm. Datasheet V2.5 says 38.59 × 38.59 × 33.5 mm (housing without mounting ears).
  • ROS/ROS2: ldrobotSensorTeam ldlidar_stl_ros / ldlidar_stl_ros2 (LD06/LD19/STL-27L).
