ADM2587E

Analog Devices communication IC. Isolated RS-485/RS-422 transceiver with a built-in isoPower DC-DC: 2.5 kVrms, 500 kbit/s, ±15 kV ESD, 256 nodes, single 3.3 or 5 V supply. Breaks ground loops on long Modbus or motor-drive runs.

Design notes

  • Single 3.0-5.5 V supply; the isoPower converter makes the isolated 3.3 V. Wire VISOOUT (pin 12) to VISOIN (pin 19)
  • Full-duplex pins (A/B receive, Y/Z drive); for half duplex tie A to Y and B to Z
  • isoPower radiates EMI: follow the ADI AN-0971 layout
  • Draws about 125 mA at 3.3 V into a 54 Ω bus load
  • ADM2582E is the 16 Mbit/s version
