W5500

WIZnet communication IC. SPI Ethernet chip with hardwired TCP/IP, MAC and integrated 10/100 PHY: gives any MCU, even one without a MAC, wired Ethernet with 8 hardware sockets. Good for UDP/TCP telemetry and control links on small robots.

Design notes

  • Hardwired TCP, UDP, IPv4, ICMP, ARP, IGMP and PPPoE; 8 sockets, 32 KB buffer
  • SPI mode 0/3; designed for 80 MHz but only 33.3 MHz is guaranteed
  • 3.3 V supply with 5 V-tolerant I/O
  • About 132 mA at a 100 Mbit/s link
  • Needs a 25 MHz crystal and external magnetics or a MagJack
